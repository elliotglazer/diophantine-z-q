The result has 214 coordinates and 188 primitive integral quadratic rows r_i. Define P_N=∑_(i=1)^188 r_i² after substituting the fixed coefficient A_N. Over Q, P_N=0 is equivalent to every r_i=0.

The exact [Laurent proof checker](construction/check_exact.py) reconstructs the deleted coordinates, checks the six inverse-power definitions, and compares all twenty factor components to the changed physical factor system up to a nonzero rational normalization and q^4. It compares all 162 untouched rows. The factors have exponents (5,2,4), with exponent 3 in the special input term. They are **new arithmetic factors**; an old factor-exponent conclusion is not reused to prove their soundness. The equations q s1=1, s2=s1², s4=s2² require q≠0. Completeness constructs witnesses in that domain. No bijection with the discarded q=0 witness stratum is claimed.

The checker verifies all 214 variables occur, degree 4, content 1, and the full quartic table. Its coefficient proof identifies the unique A_N²+1 profile and bounds every other constant or linear profile strictly below it for A_N≥149^107. Dividing those latter bounds by A_N² shows they remain strict as N increases. This proves the coefficient-height statement for N≥53 and makes no arithmetic statement about that value of N.

whenever M_j^k divides the same nonzero integer J0 for each j. No product divisibility between the scales is required. The graph-fibre argument checks the actual sum, all nonzero labels, and the published theorem's embedding condition; M2 is fixed and ineffective.
