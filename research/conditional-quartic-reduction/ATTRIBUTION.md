# Attribution and provenance

This proposal develops the construction in Elliot Glazer's
[`diophantine-z-q`](https://github.com/elliotglazer/diophantine-z-q) repository,
pinned at commit `036cebe4f1a4aa7ca89820a9b48f3629b4c364d6`.
It retains that repository's mathematical dependencies and source credits,
including Gabriel Conant's work with ChatGPT on the odd-product/point-chart
reductions and José Brox's public construction and improvements.
The repository's existing `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` remains authoritative for
its source-specific notices; copying or adapting material here does not
relicense any third-party work.

The additional research was developed in Cameron's ChatGPT session.
ChatGPT proposed and implemented the local reductions, wrote the proof notes
and construction scripts, ran exact computational checks, and used separately
assigned AI reviewers for the detector, scalar, arithmetic and coefficient
arguments. This is internal AI-assisted review, not human peer review or a
complete formal proof. No maintainer endorsement or mathematical priority is
inferred. The contributor identity and any authorship line should be agreed
by the human submitter and maintainers before publication.

Upstream copyright and attribution comments in copied/adapted code are retained.
The source lineage and exact file hashes are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json`.
Prior proof texts are preserved as research history; a historical proof's old
parameters are not silently promoted to current hypotheses.
