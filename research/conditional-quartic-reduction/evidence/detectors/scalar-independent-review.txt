1. The missing old constant path is legitimately replaced

The old all-left constant path helped force odd R=v_p(r) to be positive.
The new proof correctly replaces that step by an actual property of the
input polynomial, not an unsupported assertion for arbitrary rational beta.
The source really has
  beta=11016 X^2+11016 X+5155, 11016=2^3*3^4*17,
  r=beta(beta-1)(32beta+343).
Outside S={2,3,5,7,13,17}, if v_p(X)<0 the quadratic term has uniquely
smallest valuation, hence v_p(beta)=2v_p(X) and v_p(r)=6v_p(X), an even
integer. If X is integral at p, all three factors of r are integral.
Therefore odd R is positive. I independently reconstructed all these
actual source definitions; the argument would fail for an independently
chosen rational beta, which the source does not permit.

2. Algebra, domains and quadratic implementation

Write A=f(H)_0, B=f(H)_1, C=f(Q)_0 and D=f(Q)_1. The changed nodes are
  n=B^2/r^2+C^2/r,
  L=A^2/r+n^2/r^2,
  J=D^2+r^-15*y^2,
  N=L^2+r^-15*J^2, W=1+N,
with y=T/r^16 and T=Re(k_H w) retained exactly.
The actual four left-side coordinates are z=B/r, n, u=n/r, and L, with
  rn=zB+C^2, rL=A^2+un.
Both later right-hand squares are explicitly materialized. The source
therefore implements quadratic equations, rather than inlining a cubic.
The old n0 coordinate has disappeared, and W=1+N is an affine alias.

Since beta>=2401, r>0. Every denominator is a power of this positive r;
all new extensions are unique. N>=0 and W>=1 at the real place. The
existing absorption of W^2 in the normalized pole witnesses remains
legitimate. The semantic pole input remains exactly 42rW^2, and all
normalized conic/preparation rows were checked separately. Point charts
are unchanged: the inverse of f(Q)_0 still excludes Q=O or S=P, while
the allowed tangent case S=-P and every chart sign choice remain.

3. The new valuation argument handles cancellation correctly

The scalar pole gives v(W)>-R/2. The preceding input-polynomial argument
gives R>0, so v(1)=0>-R/2. Hence N=W-1 also has v(N)>-R/2 by the
ultrametric inequality. This step allows cancellation in 1+N; no parity
claim is made about that affine addition.

For every binary norm node the coefficient valuations have opposite
parity: (-2R,-R) for n, (-R,-2R) for L, and (0,-15R) for J and N.
Consequently exact valuation descent gives
  v(L)>-R/4, v(J)>29R/4,
  v(A)>3R/8, v(n)>7R/8,
  v(B)>23R/16, v(C)>15R/16,
  v(D)>29R/8, v(y)>89R/8,
  v(T)>217R/8.
The attached independent checker recomputes all ten affine bounds with
exact rational arithmetic. Zero arguments are harmless under v(0)=infinity.

All four point coefficients have positive integer valuation before the
retained proxy lemma is used, so both points are formal at all places
above the consumed prime. The same two-place proxy lemma gives
v_p(T)=v_p((a-1)/h), including the nonunit case at 41. Since h is a
nonzero integer, uniform v_p(a-1)>=28 follows. This is the required
radical divisibility M_S(beta)^28 | a-1. The proof does not assert the
stronger v_p(T)>=28R, which the strict 217R/8 bound does not generally give.

4. Completeness and choice order

At p|r with R>=1, require
  v(A)>=R+1, v(B),v(C)>=2R+1,
  v(D)>=4R+1, v(y)>=12R+1.
Direct substitution gives
  v(n)>=2R+2, v(L)>=R+2, v(J)>=8R+2,
  v(N)>=R+4, W=1 modulo p.
The y condition is the finite bound v(T)>=28R+1. The old preparation can
impose these requirements: choose H sufficiently deep first, then Q
sufficiently deeper. For fixed H, as Q tends to O the point S tends to P,
h_S stays bounded, w=h_S*tau tends to zero, and T tends to zero.
No conflict between primary and alternate chosen-prime indices is added.

At p|42 with p not dividing r, the weights are units; requiring positive
valuation of the four coefficients and y gives N=0 and W=1 modulo p.
At every other prime the extra factor W^2 contributes even valuation.
Thus the same square reduced denominator and local squareclasses of
42rW^2 are available. These preparation arguments use deep formal
neighborhoods at bad or ramified primes, not a good-reduction argument.
After S is fixed, the changed node functions have nonzero denominators
at the prepared H; continuity preserves finite lower bounds on a suitable
congruence class of h. The roots and scales remain fixed before later
contact-prime selection. This proves the needed existential transfer,
without a purported bijection between old and new pole witness tuples.
