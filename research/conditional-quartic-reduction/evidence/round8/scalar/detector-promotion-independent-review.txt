Subject
-------

Soundness
---------
The retained arbitrary-positive-order one-place lemma gives the new requested
orders simultaneously at one common place. In particular its new order12
conclusion for g(U_A) supplies the old order1 premise. Every other semantic
test and order is unchanged. No equality U_A=[n]H is assumed in this direction;
that is a completeness choice, not a premise for arbitrary solutions. The
stronger premise therefore does not weaken the retained input comparison,
G2/G12 approximation, scalar, Lucas or height consumers. The identity primary
case remains represented by g=0,l=+/-1 and satisfies every positive order.
