ROUND-EIGHT TWO-PRIME DIAGONAL CHANGE: INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Disposition: ACCEPT as an exact transformation of the supplied finite system.

Reviewed object
---------------
The integrated source declares S=3^2364 and contains 389 rational coordinates
and 357 quadratic residuals after compilation. The new map changes thirteen
existing coordinates, fixes the distinguished input, and introduces no new
variable. The source, producer, proof, independent checker, generated files,
positive report and negative-probe report are bound by the companion JSON.

The proof reviewed here is diagonal_scaling_round8.tex, not the historical
round-seven diagonal_scaling.tex. The independently authored checker imports
neither the producer, builder, compiler, nor another checker. Its expected
exponent and measured-count claims are supplied explicitly on its command line.

Exact map and inverse
---------------------
Let c=3^394. Six stored coordinates have the old value c times their new
value: the two h_S coefficients, the two stored x(S) coefficients, and the
two f(S-P) coefficients. The stored real x(S) coefficient additionally has
a factor 2. The two stored w=h_S*tau coefficients have factor c^2. The three
translated anchor witnesses and the physical anchor have factor c^4. The
stored heightProduct.scale0.quadratic coordinate has factor 2^-21. Every
other coordinate, including X, is fixed. The listed union has thirteen
coordinates: twelve with a ternary factor and two with a dyadic factor.

Every factor is an explicit nonzero rational number. Dividing by these
factors gives a rational inverse on the entire 389-dimensional space; there
is no square-root existence condition. In particular it would be incorrect
to reinterpret the new coordinates directly as the old point coordinates.
Their semantic values are obtained by decoding the complete diagonal map.

The square-scale detail has been checked against the actual raw value map,
not inferred from a coordinate name. In this source heightProduct.0 is
2*x236 and heightProduct.scale0 is 4*x238, while the row named
heightProduct.scale0.definition is x238-x236^2=0. Thus the old semantic
height scale A=C0^2 equals 4*x238. After x238=2^-21*y238 it is
A=2^-19*y238, so y238=2^19*C0^2 need not be a rational square. The new proof
states this correctly. Detector square-scale and parity arguments apply to
decoded A=C0^2, which satisfies the original rows, and require no square
claim about y238 itself. The same decoding principle recovers x(S)=h_S^2.

Row normalization and existential projection
--------------------------------------------
For each old integer quadratic row, substitute the exact rational map and
the fixed parameter. Let L be the positive least common denominator and g
the signed integer content after multiplication by L. The divisor d=g/L
is nonzero, and dividing the substituted row by d gives a primitive integer
quadratic. Rowwise division by a nonzero rational preserves every equation.

The independent checker reconstructs this normalization by a different
algorithm: divide all coefficients by the leading rational coefficient p,
take the common denominator M of these ratios, and use d=p/M. It checks
primitive content and exact proportionality for all 357 rows. It confirms
that exactly five divisors are noninteger, with denominators 2^21 in three
rows, 2^42 in one row and 2^13 in one row. In particular an integer-content
certificate without a denominator field would not suffice for this map.

The new polynomial is exactly the sum of the squares of these 357 primitive
rows. Rational squares are nonnegative in the usual ordering, so the sum
vanishes exactly when every row vanishes. Together with the diagonal
bijection fixing X, this proves equality of the old and new existential
projections onto X. It does not assert that the old and new sums of squares
are constant multiples of one another, nor is that assertion needed.

Complete finite checks
----------------------
The checker re-expanded every normalized quadratic by unordered term pairs,
including the doubled off-diagonal products. It compared all coefficients
against both the produced JSON polynomial and the delivered decimal table.
All 389 variables occur, the exact degree is four, the ordinary support has
26,295 monomials, the integer content is one, and the coefficient of X^4
is one. The table is 1,050,826 bytes and has SHA-256
1c612d20915667dfd81a7895f8d93231d5efeaf86ecfaa15194408d298d35573.

All 357 substituted source rows have c-exponent span at most two: 335 have
span zero, 11 have span one and 11 have span two. The exact maximum after
full expansion is 28902*c^4=28902*3^1576, with 757 decimal digits and
2513 bits. This is an exact measured assertion at the selected parameter;
no floating-point optimizer or unproved minimization claim enters its audit.

As a distinct probe, the same change applied to the frozen 389-coordinate
source at the previous exponent 2790 gives 28902*3^1860, or 892 digits and
2963 bits. This corroborates the proof's separation between the small-prime
balancing improvement and the further exponent reduction supplied by the
arithmetic argument. Neither probe justifies the arithmetic argument itself.

Six targeted negative probes all failed for the expected reason: an incorrect
anchor exponent; an incorrect expected coordinate count; a changed dyadic
weight; a changed fractional row divisor; a changed polynomial coefficient
with its certificate hash rebound; and a changed decimal-table coefficient.
The rebound-polynomial probe demonstrates an algebraic comparison beyond
mere digest matching. These probes were run on isolated copies, not on the
accepted integrated output.

Integrated detector conservation
-------------------------------
The separately authored detector checker was also rerun on the integrated
source with --expected-exponent 2364 and a regenerated round-seven baseline.
Its 157 assertions passed. It compared all 362 unaffected symbolic equations
by semantic coordinate name, bound the same primary-order promotion proof,
and confirmed exactly the three deleted q1*q12 coordinates, raw 400/368 and
compiled 389/357. The parameter change therefore does not hide an additional
symbolic detector change. The prior independent formal-group/completeness
review remains applicable to this same symbolic replacement.

Scope and remaining dependencies
--------------------------------
No material defect was found in the new finite transformation or its proof.
This review establishes the input-fixed diagonal bijection, nonzero row
normalization, full SOS identity and exact coefficient/count assertions for
the bound files. The original source-to-core compilation is checked by its
own gate and compiler audits. The choice S=3^2364 is justified by the separate
arithmetic proof and its review. The identification of the original source's
projection with Z retains the global arithmetic qualifications in the package.
This is internal AI-assisted review, not external peer review, a complete
Lean certification, a minimality proof or a publication decision.
