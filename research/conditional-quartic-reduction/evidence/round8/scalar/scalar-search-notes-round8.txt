ROUND-EIGHT SCALAR/GEOMETRY SEARCH: NO ADDITIONAL REDUCTION SELECTED

The retained outer-constant scalar tree remains selected. The following
avenues were examined before the independent detector and diagonal reviews
were prioritized. None is counted as a variable saving.

1. Two Laurent-power steps give r^-7 and uniform scalar order14 in the retained
   five-leaf tree. The proposed height exponent37/2 still exceeds14. Thus the
   simpler anchor arithmetic does not rescue that immediate one-coordinate
   deletion. Three steps give r^-15 and scalar order28 as before.

2. The shared k_V output is consumed only by the common alternate logarithm,
   suggesting direct substitution of k_V=(xF+js-1)/(hs). Directly multiplying
   the final G12 polynomial by hs makes its previously quadratic numerator
   cubic unless its two rational polynomial-output coordinates are retained.
   That compensating cost prevents a saving in this straightforward fusion.
   No complete alternative evaluation circuit with a lower count was found;
   no compiled count is claimed for this idea.

3. Replacing the two coefficients of f(S-P) by the stored w coordinates could
   remove a field quotient, but small w can arise from S approaching the
   two-torsion point(0,0), not only from S approaching P. Without an additional
   argument it does not supply the formal Q=S-P premise needed by the scalar
   trace proxy. A norm-based replacement would have to control branches and
   the different possible divisibility factors a and a-1; that may consume
   more scalar precision and gates than it saves. This is an unresolved
   alternative, not a proof that every such approach is impossible.

4. Fixing a private pole witness, for example e=17, would make one conic easy
   but no completeness theorem for the remaining quartic/conic system was
   obtained. The source pole-realization theorem does not automatically
   permit this specialization. No such deletion is accepted or implemented.

No mathematical defect in the selected scalar argument was identified by
these searches. The indispensable shifted-point nonzero guard, both-place
formal premises, split41 case and current preparation order are retained.
