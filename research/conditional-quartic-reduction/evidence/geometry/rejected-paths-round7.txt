ROUND-SEVEN GEOMETRY SEARCH RECORD

Selected and measured
---------------------
Moving the constant outside the scalar norm tree is selected. It relies on the
actual input polynomial to exclude negative odd contact valuations, rather than
an inner constant path. The full isolated pipeline is 409/377 -> 399/367 -> 395/363.
Its scalar order remains 28. The rigorous transfer, source reconstruction, and
hash bindings are in geometry_round7.tex, check_geometry_round7.py, and the
associated reports. The actual earlier source already notes that a negative
valuation of beta is twice that of X (addition.tex 421–433); the current argument
checks that property for the changed beta and contact product and uses it to
simplify the circuit. No historical arithmetic gap is being alleged.

Other avenues considered but not selected
-----------------------------------------
1. Replacing the three inverse-power steps by two within the existing uniform
   family gives scalar order 14. The unchanged exponent 19 height estimate cannot
   make c^(1−19/14)>80H by enlarging the anchor. This is an obstruction within
   that family and consumer, not a lower bound for every possible scalar gadget.
2. Replacing weighted binary norms by unweighted sums of two squares would
   lose uniform noncancellation. For example 1^2+12^2 is divisible by 29 even
   though both entries are 29-adic units; 29 is outside the exceptional set.
   This invalidates the proposed local inference for arbitrary rational leaves.
   It is not a claimed counterexample to the whole elliptic construction.
3. Using small w=h_S*tau directly instead of both coefficients of f(S−P)
   requires an additional exclusion of a reduction where h_S is small. No
   uniform exclusion on the actual cyclic point domain was proved, so this
   branch was not implemented or counted as a saving.
4. Removing the rational inverse of f(S−P)_0 would reopen the identity output
   S=P at the point-chart level. No proof that the other retained consumers
   exclude that branch was found. The guard is retained; there is no claim of
   a constructed global counterexample when it is omitted.
5. Dropping the final affine 1 from the selected W gives no coordinate saving
   and loses the automatic real bound W>=1 and the convenient W=1 modulo the
   preparation primes. The free affine constant is retained.
6. An inverse outer pole base and rescaled constant can redistribute valuation
   bounds, but the explored four-node realizations still require the same number
   of scalar coordinates. No uncompiled variant is claimed to improve the count.

The scalar search was constructive and limited, not exhaustive. Only the selected
candidate's complete compiler measurement is reported as a new reduction.
