# Evidence and its scope

The copied JSON/text receipts in this directory record internal AI-assisted
reviews and exact computations on the supplied source. They do not certify
the inherited global arithmetic or substitute for reading the proofs.

`review-bindings.json` maps each preserved review artifact to its original
local filename and hash. Some original receipts contain historical local
paths; those are provenance strings, not runtime dependencies. Follow the
portable file mapping and `PROVENANCE.json` to locate the actual reviewed
source in this proposal. The original receipts have not been rewritten to
make their hashes or scopes appear more current.

The detector proof received exactly four one-space editorial insertions
after its original review. The original snapshot, exact patch, addendum and
rerun report preserve that transition. All non-whitespace bytes are identical;
the final selected proof has a new hash. The addendum binds that hash without
erasing the earlier review.

The full table and diagonal coefficient checks are rerun by `../reproduce.py`.
Generated output/audit reports live in its fresh output directory, not in this
evidence folder. The supplied receipts are reference evidence; they cannot
seed the build or satisfy the wrapper's fresh-output checks.

The optional odd-anchor/projective-height note has its own proof review and
finite checks. It is excluded from the selected construction's dependency
chain. The rejected-path notes record limits of the searches; they do not
establish a global variable or gate lower bound.
