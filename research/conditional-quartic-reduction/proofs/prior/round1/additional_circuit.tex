\subsection{Feed the scaled scalar ratio directly into the norm tree}
The scalar point is $R=Q_2-P=(x_R,y_R)$ and $r_\beta\ne0$ is the retained
scalar contact product. Write $t=r_\beta^{-32}$. The old source computes
\[
 z_R=-x_R/y_R,\qquad \eta=-z_R k,\qquad (\lambda_4,\lambda_5)
       =\text{the two rational components of }\eta t.
\]
It uses $z_R$ and $\eta$ only for these two leaves. Reuse the shared $k$
from the preceding subsection, introduce $k_t=kt\in K$, and define
$\Lambda\in K$ directly by
\[
 y_R\Lambda=x_Rk_t.
\]
The inherited point-domain statement gives $y_R\ne0$, so
$\Lambda=x_Rkt/y_R=\eta t$. Use its rational components as the two
leaves. Six rational coordinates for $z_R,\eta,\eta t$ become four for
$k_t,\Lambda$, saving two coordinates after final compilation. Every
displayed field equation remains quadratic in the existing coordinates.

The net savings, after compilation, are therefore
\[
\begin{array}{lr}
\text{Grouped products}&14\\
\text{Primary ratios}&8\\
\text{Scalar leaves}&2\\
\text{Packed parity and anchor}&16
\end{array}
\]
giving $523-14-8-2-16=483$ total variables.
