SCALAR PREPARATION CONIC

With beta=1224X^2+2451 and X rational, beta>=2451>2401. Therefore each
factor of r=beta(beta-1)(beta-2401)(32beta+343) is positive, so
r>0. The scalar norm recursion has
 n0=1+f_U0,0^2/r>=1,
 nLeft=n0^2+r*n1^2>=1,
 W=nLeft^2+r*nRight^2>=1.
These inequalities hold for every rational solution of the circuit equations.
Consequently b=21*r*W^2>0. No local arithmetic claim is needed for this sign.

The old preparation is T=(1+b*t^2)/b and U=2/T-b. Hence
 U=b(1-b*t^2)/(1+b*t^2).
Introduce V=2*b*t/(1+b*t^2). Direct multiplication gives
 U^2+b*V^2=b^2.
All quantities are rational; denominators are nonzero because b>0.

Conversely, let U,V be any rational solution of U^2+b*V^2=b^2. If U!=-b,
put t=V/(b+U); direct substitution recovers the displayed Mobius formula.
If U=-b, the conic and b>0 give V=0. Replace U by +b; all other retained
residuals are unchanged because they use U only through U^2. Then t=0
recovers the old preparation. This is equivalence after existential
projection, not a bijection at that exceptional pair.

Implementation: two base coordinates U,V replace one old base t. Keep a
quadratic assignment for each of U^2,V^2 and impose
 U2 + b*V2 - b^2=0.
The downstream expression Bh=e*(17b-U2) is unchanged. The old t^2,T,U+b
assignments disappear. Net variable saving is 1. All residuals remain
quadratic. The combined raw-structure checker verifies that the only raw
residual occurrence of U is its square definition, so the exceptional sign
flip is mechanically certified to preserve all other equations.

SEMANTIC b COORDINATE

The scalar b output is stored directly as one coordinate, with its full
quadratic defining expression b-21*r*W2=0, where W2 denotes the already
stored W^2 expression. This replaces the old normalized homogeneous-gate
coordinate by an invertible affine change. No coordinate is added. It avoids
raising a large normalization coefficient to the fourth power in the conic
contribution to the SOS.
