\section{Positive detector products from an existing Gaussian contact}

Add two rational witnesses $a_+,b_+$ and one quadratic equation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:positive-lambda}
 \lambda-\frac1{2399}=a_+^2+b_+^2.
\end{equation}
This restriction is supplied by the existing completeness construction.
It makes three trace-inverse guards unnecessary, for a net saving of one
variable. Arbitrary old witness tuples need not extend; the argument below
establishes both the soundness implication and canonical completeness.

\subsection{Domains from positivity}
At either real embedding $\sigma$, every root chart satisfies
$x_\sigma\ge\sqrt{D_\sigma}=3-\sigma(\rho)$. Hence for the four-abscissa
product $C_0+C_1\rho$,
\[
 C_0\ge\frac{(3-\rho)^4+(3+\rho)^4}{2}=193.
\]
All three detector scales $A\in\{1,C_0^2,(1+C_1^2)^2\}$ are at least one.
Put $t=A\lambda$, $\delta=t-1$. Equation~\eqref{eq:positive-lambda} gives
$t\ge1/2399$, so
\[
 F(t)=t(t+1/2)(t+1)(t-1/2399)(t+32/407)\ge0.
\]
Retain the existing weight relations
\[
 F(t)e=(A\delta)^5,\qquad \delta^3q_1=e,\qquad ee_{-1}=1.
\]
The last relation gives $e\ne0$, then the middle gives $\delta\ne0$, and
the first gives $F(t)>0$. Thus
\[
 q_1=A^5\delta^2/F(t)>0,\qquad q_{40}=q_1e^{78}>0.
\]
The unchanged power chain gives the second identity exactly.

For the semantic detector product
\[
 W=\prod_{j=1}^{16}(1+q_{m_j}D_j^2)=w_0+w_1\rho,
 \qquad m_j\in\{1,40\},
\]
every factor is at least one at both real embeddings. Consequently
$\sigma(W)\ge1$, $w_0\ge1$, and $\Norm(W)>0$. Delete the three rational
inverse witnesses for $w_0$. The retained norm equation now uniquely gives
\[
 n=\frac{w_0^2-2w_1^2}{2w_0}>0.
\]
Since $e\delta=A^5\delta^6/F(t)>0$, the pole input
$b=n^2/(e\delta)$ is positive as well. These domain conclusions hold
before any pole equation is used. Deleted witnesses recover uniquely as
$1/w_0$; no zero-over-zero solution is introduced.

\subsection{Why completeness supplies the two witnesses}
The actual source prime-selection passage in
\texttt{manuscripts/quartic\_v10.tex} chooses
\[
 v=D_0^2w^2,\qquad\lambda=u/v,
\]
after fixing the scales, with $D_0^2\theta/A$ integral for every contact.
The contact $A=1$, $\theta=1/2399$ is retained. Its prime form is
\[
 p=u-v/2399=\Norm_{\Q(i)/\Q}(\pi),
\]
where the same simultaneous-prime theorem supplies a Gaussian generator
$\pi=A_0+iB_0$. Repeated forms are merged with their incidences retained,
so this contact still has a selected prime value. Set
\[
 a_+=A_0/(D_0w),\qquad b_+=B_0/(D_0w).
\]
Their squared sum is $p/v=\lambda-1/2399$. Thus the existing completeness
witnesses extend to~\eqref{eq:positive-lambda}, with no stronger prime
theorem or additional thin-set restriction. The source already chooses
positive sufficiently large $\lambda$ and nonzero $D_0w$.

In isolation this adds two coordinates and one residual, removes three
coordinates and three residuals, and gives 449 variables and 417 residuals.
The direct positivity proof also simplifies all subsequent pole domains.
