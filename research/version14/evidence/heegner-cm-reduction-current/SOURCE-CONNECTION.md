# Generic-conductor inert CM reduction

Author: /root/v14_arithmetic_author. Bounded source application; pending independent review and additive source adoption.

## Published statement

Gross and Zagier, On singular moduli, Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik355(1985),191–220, printed198 defines j=j(tau), O=Z[tau] and the discriminant Delta of this imaginary quadratic argument. The opening paragraph of printed199 states that (Delta/ell)=-1 gives supersingular reduction at every prime above ell in K(j). This is the general forward reduction statement; the later explicit congruences for small ell and the later fundamental-discriminant lifting theorem are not used.

## Actual order and prime

The current Heegner curve attached to a proper O_d ideal has endomorphism order O_d=Z+d O_K of discriminant Delta=d^2 D_K. Every permitted new prime ell lies outside6dND_K and is inert in K. Hence ell is odd and does not divide dD_K, and (Delta/ell)=(d^2/ell)(D_K/ell)=-1. The cited statement therefore gives supersingular geometric reduction of this CM elliptic curve at the chosen prime above ell, for every permitted old conductor d, not just d=1 or an inert squarefree conductor.

Changing the proper ideal class changes the CM j-invariant within the same order discriminant. The same calculation and the source statement at every prime apply after every such ideal-class translate, with its chosen place transported. Passing to a finite local field extension to realize a good model does not change the geometric supersingular reduction.
