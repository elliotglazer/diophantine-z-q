# Semisimplicity after coefficient extension

Let V be a finite-dimensional semisimple representation of a group over a characteristic-zero field K. Then V remains semisimple after any algebraic extension of K. This proof uses the separately proved central-simple splitting step in the centralizer provider.

Let R be the K-linear span of the image matrices. It is a finite-dimensional unital algebra, and its submodules in V are exactly the group-stable subspaces. Evaluation on a K-basis of V embeds the left regular module R into a finite direct sum of copies of V. A submodule of a finite sum of simple modules is a direct summand: choose a maximal direct sum of simple summands disjoint from the submodule. If its sum with that submodule is proper, a further simple summand outside the sum can be added. The submodule is then a quotient of the original semisimple module, and the images of its simple summands make it semisimple. In particular the regular module R is semisimple.

Decompose this regular module into isomorphism classes of simple summands. Between distinct simple classes every module homomorphism is zero, and the endomorphisms of a simple summand form a division algebra. Thus End_R(R), which is R with multiplication reversed, is a finite product of matrix algebras over finite-dimensional division algebras. Each division algebra has a center finite and separable over K. Extending to an algebraic closure of K splits these centers into their field embeddings and splits each central division algebra into matrices by the stated central-simple result. Hence the extended R is a product of matrix algebras over the algebraic closure. Modules for it are semisimple by the central idempotents and diagonal matrix units.

For an intermediate algebraic extension L, let W be an R_L-stable subspace of V_L. Over the algebraic closure there is an R-equivariant projection onto the extended W. The conditions on a projection to have image in W, restrict to the identity on W, and commute with a basis of R are linear equations over L. Their solvability over the algebraic closure implies solvability over L by row reduction. Thus W has a stable complement over L. This proves the assertion for the actual finite splitting coefficient fields and for the algebraic closure used to establish absolute irreducibility.

In each Faltings application, apply this result to the semisimple rational Tate module over its actual endomorphism field. The separately computed scalar commutant of its matrix-unit summand then forces irreducibility over the algebraic closure. The qualitative comparison field in the lemma and the original controlled fields in its two direct applications remain distinct.
