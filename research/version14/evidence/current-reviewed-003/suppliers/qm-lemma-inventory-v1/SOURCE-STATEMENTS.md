# Selected published source statements

These authored projections preserve the scope of the exact sources. Full source files remain outside this packet.

## QM-LEMMA-WEIL

Weil restriction of an abelian variety through a finite separable extension exists and becomes the product of its conjugates after a splitting extension.

## QM-LEMMA-HOM-TORSION

The homomorphism group of abelian varieties is torsion-free.

## QM-LEMMA-POINCARE

Poincare reducibility over the perfect ground field gives complementary factors up to isogeny and a semisimple rational endomorphism algebra.

## QM-LEMMA-ICM

A Q-simple abelian variety of primitive GL2-type is isogenous to a Q-simple factor of J_1(N) for some N.

## QM-LEMMA-POINCARE-H1

A Q-defined abelian subvariety has a complementary factor up to isogeny over Q.

## QM-LEMMA-CLASSICAL

The classical parabolic degree-one tower cohomology in the selected weight-two case is a direct sum of the two-dimensional cohomological representations V_f indexed by cuspidal newforms, with their multiplicity spaces.

## QM-LEMMA-DUAL

The cohomological representation uses the source geometric-Frobenius convention for its displayed Hecke polynomial.

## QM-LEMMA-FALTINGS

For an abelian variety A over a number field K, V_l(A) is semisimple and the natural map End_K(A) tensor Z_l to End_{G_K}(T_l(A)) is an isomorphism.

## QM-LEMMA-KRONECKER-WEBER

Every finite abelian extension of Q is contained in a cyclotomic extension.

## QM-LEMMA-SHIMURA-TWIST

The primitive-character weighted translate is the nonzero Gauss multiplier times the coefficientwise character twist and belongs to the weight-two cusp space on Gamma_1(r^2 M).

## QM-LEMMA-LI-NEWFORM

A nonzero simultaneous prime-to-level eigenform in the given cusp space has a primitive newform source with the same good-prime eigenvalues by the old/new decomposition.

## QM-LEMMA-POINCARE-SIMPLE

A Q-defined abelian subvariety has a complementary factor up to isogeny over Q.

## QM-LEMMA-LI-NORMALIZE

A nonzero newform has nonzero first Fourier coefficient and can be scaled to first coefficient one.

## QM-LEMMA-LI-HECKE-NORMALIZATION

Li uses the lower-right-entry character convention at Gamma_0(L). The displayed prime-to-level relation for a newform has coefficient expression c_(pn)+eta(p)p^(k-1)c_(n/p).
