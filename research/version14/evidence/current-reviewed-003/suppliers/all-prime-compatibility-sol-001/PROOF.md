# Compatibility of each original family at every coefficient prime

Fix one of the two original families. For the first use eta=beta, and for the second use eta=b. Keep its own original abelian surface A, maps mu_sigma, positive scalars a_sigma, common definition field L-prime, and normalized representations rho_ell=eta(sigma)^(-1)a_sigma mu_sigma composed with sigma. No identification between the two families or between their fields is made. Retain the original coefficient-field idempotent defining r_ell in rho_ell=r_ell direct-sum r_ell. Assume the full geometric endomorphism algebra and common definition field hypotheses and the normalized-family identities required by the separate all-prime irreducibility argument. Retain the accepted prime-five modularity conclusion for this family, with its actual primitive weight-two form f and original r_5.

Fix any rational coefficient prime ell. Enlarge the family's algebraic coefficient field to contain the coefficients of f. Retain its original embedding at five and extend the original embedding at ell separately to this common field. For a cofinite set of rational primes p, exclude 5, ell, the bad reduction and field ramification primes, the finitely many denominators and primes needed to spread the finite isogeny family, and the primes dividing the as-yet-unbounded level of f. This is only a comparison exception set. It does not enlarge the support T in any conductor estimate or change L-prime.

Choose arithmetic Frobenius sigma_p in the decomposition group at such a p. Its residue action is x mapped to x^p. The relative p-Frobenius of the reduction of A has target the reduction of its sigma_p twist. Compose it with the reduction of m mu_sigma_p, where a single positive integer m clears denominators for the finite family. This gives an actual endomorphism e_p of the reduction of A. Milne's degree-polynomial theorem gives a rational Tate-prime-independent trace of e_p. Functorial prime-to-p torsion specialization identifies the action of e_p/m with the reduction of mu_sigma_p composed with arithmetic Frobenius. Consequently the trace of r_ell(sigma_p) is the ell-adic image of the same algebraic number a_sigma_p eta(sigma_p)^(-1) Tr(e_p)/(2m). The factor two comes from the two copies of the original rank-two factor. This argument is the rational-endomorphism and twist-source calculation of the preserved prime-five-to-three proof, with ell in place of three.

At coefficient five this algebraic number equals the good-prime eigenvalue a_p(f), by the retained identification of the original r_5 with f. Injectivity of the chosen five-adic embedding makes this an equality in the common algebraic coefficient field. The independently extended ell-adic embedding therefore gives the good-prime trace of the classical two-dimensional realization U_f,ell. The classical good-prime trace statement allows every rational coefficient prime, independently of ordinarity. Equal traces on this cofinite Frobenius set extend to equal traces on G_Q by continuity and Chebotarev. The characteristic-zero trace criterion then gives r_ell isomorphic to the semisimplification of U_f,ell.

The separate all-prime irreducibility argument makes this original r_ell absolutely irreducible. Thus the semisimplification of U_f,ell has one simple composition factor. A nonzero proper invariant subspace of U_f,ell would give both a nonzero subrepresentation and a nonzero quotient, hence at least two composition factors. Therefore U_f,ell itself is irreducible and isomorphic to its semisimplification, and hence to the original r_ell. This is the same finite-length deduction already independently reviewed at ell=3, now with the coefficient prime universally quantified.

The conclusion is available for each fixed family and each rational ell, with the corresponding embedding of the same form f. For a local residue characteristic p one may select an auxiliary ell different from p, including at p=3 or p=5. This supplies equality of the actual local Galois representations after restriction. The comparison between the conductor of that realization and the primitive modular level remains a separate Carayol/Casselman source connection. No conductor estimate, bound on the comparison exception set, integral lattice assertion or new field-degree bound is proved here. The previous r3-only proof and its reviews remain unchanged.
