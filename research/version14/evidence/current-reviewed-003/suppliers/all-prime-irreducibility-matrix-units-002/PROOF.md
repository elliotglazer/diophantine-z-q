# Absolute irreducibility of every original coefficient-prime factor

Fix any rational prime ell, including ell=2, and the original finite extension E_lambda of Q_ell used to split B in the construction of rho_ell. Put H=G_{L-prime} and V=V_ell(A). Retain the same abelian surface A and the same number field L-prime for every ell. Assume all geometric rational endomorphisms of A are defined over L-prime and End^0(A_Qbar)=B. Faltings gives semisimplicity of V for this ell. Rationalizing its integral endomorphism comparison gives End_H(V)=B tensor_Q Q_ell.

The retained characteristic-zero coefficient-extension lemma makes W=V tensor_Qell E_lambda semisimple. Commutation with H is a homogeneous linear system on the finite-dimensional endomorphism space. Finitely many equations span all its constraints, so their common kernel commutes with field extension. Hence End_H(W)=B tensor_Q E_lambda=M_2(E_lambda).

Use the original normalized family and its original matrix units in B tensor_Q E_lambda. Let e=e_11 be their primitive idempotent. Its image is the original factor r_ell. If another splitting is used for the calculation, its matrix units are conjugate to the original ones by an invertible element of B tensor_Q E_lambda; this element commutes with the normalized G_Q-action and therefore identifies the two factors G_Q-equivariantly. On H the original normalized family is the ordinary Tate action. Thus W is E_lambda^2 tensor R, where R=r_ell|H, and End_H(W)=M_2(End_H(R)). Comparing dimensions with M_2(E_lambda) gives End_H(R)=E_lambda. The factor R is a direct summand of semisimple W, so it is semisimple.

For every finite extension F/E_lambda the coefficient-extension lemma preserves semisimplicity, and the same finite-kernel argument gives End_H(R tensor_Elambda F)=F. A proper nonzero invariant summand would give a nontrivial idempotent in this field. Thus R remains irreducible after every finite extension. A proper invariant subspace over an algebraic closure descends to a finite extension. Choose a basis with finitely many algebraic coordinates, and use the intersection with the resulting finite-field vector space to check invariance. Hence R is absolutely irreducible. Every G_Q-invariant subspace is H-invariant, so the original r_ell is absolutely irreducible on G_Q.

Since ell was arbitrary, the conclusion holds for every rational coefficient prime with its original splitting field and the same original normalized family. The full geometric endomorphism algebra, its common field of definition, and the normalized-family restriction remain separate incoming hypotheses. Compatible normalizer isogenies alone do not supply these inputs. The earlier prime-three proof and its reviews remain unchanged. Local ordinarity, residual properties, de Rham comparisons, ramification support, conductor estimates and field-degree bounds remain outside this argument.
