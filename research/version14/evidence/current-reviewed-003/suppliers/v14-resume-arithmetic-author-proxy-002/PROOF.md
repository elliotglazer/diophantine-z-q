## Matrix units and all coefficient primes

Lines 1357-1375 choose a single number field C splitting B and containing all a_sigma and beta values, and define rho_ell for every rational ell. Fix matrix units in B tensor C and transport them along the chosen embedding into a finite coefficient field F_ell. Write e=e_11 and R_ell=e(V_ell(A) tensor F_ell). Since rho_ell commutes with B, R_ell is G_Q-stable and is the original factor r_ell up to G_Q-isomorphism. If the originally chosen matrix units were selected separately at ell, any two such systems in M_2(F_ell) are conjugate by an invertible element of B tensor F_ell. That element commutes with rho_ell and gives the required G_Q-isomorphism between the primitive images. Thus fixing units over C does not change the original representation. The other diagonal image is carried isomorphically to R_ell by e_12 and e_21, so rho_ell=R_ell direct-sum R_ell as G_Q representations.

There is a small interface distinction from the conditional all-prime-irreducibility-sol-001 proof. Its third paragraph names a previously embedded quadratic coefficient field E. The first beta-family does not name such an E. No such field is needed: its own matrix units give the primitive idempotent just used. The proof below provides the precise beta-family specialization and does not introduce E as a new assumption.

Put H=G_L'. Since L' defines all geometric endomorphisms and End^0(A_Qbar)=B, Faltings gives a semisimple rational Tate representation V and End_H(V)=B tensor Q_ell. Commutation is a system of homogeneous linear equations on a finite-dimensional endomorphism space; finitely many span all the equations. Taking this kernel commutes with coefficient extension. Characteristic-zero semisimplicity likewise persists under finite coefficient extension. Thus W=V tensor F_ell is semisimple and End_H(W)=M_2(F_ell).

The same matrix units identify W with F_ell^2 tensor R_ell. Hence End_H(W)=M_2(End_H(R_ell)); comparison of dimensions gives End_H(R_ell)=F_ell. The summand R_ell is semisimple. For each finite extension F/F_ell the same kernel argument and characteristic-zero coefficient-extension result give semisimplicity and scalar endomorphism ring F. A proper nonzero invariant summand would give a nontrivial idempotent in F, a contradiction. Every invariant subspace over an algebraic closure descends to a finite extension by choosing a basis and adjoining its finitely many coordinates. Therefore R_ell|H is absolutely irreducible, and so is R_ell on G_Q. This applies to every rational ell, including 2. No integral-lattice or residual assertion is made.
