# Selected published statements

These short authored representations preserve the selected source versions.

## CALEGARI

A continuous irreducible odd geometric two-dimensional G_Q representation at p at least five with distinct Hodge-Tate weights is modular up to twist.

## SCHOLZE

Proper-smooth p-adic comparison identifies etale and de Rham cohomology after the de Rham period extension, with its filtration.

## FONTAINE

The admissible representation category has exact tensor, dual and subobject closure, compatible with its filtered period functor.

## MILNE-HODGE

Complex abelian varieties have their functorial lattice uniformization and rational Hodge description.

## GV-DETERMINANT

For a classical cuspidal eigenform of weight k at least two, its representation has the stated good-prime polynomial and determinant nebentype times cyclotomic character to k-1.
