# Selected source statements

## Milne degree polynomial

For an abelian variety D over a field k and a rational endomorphism alpha, the rational degree polynomial is the characteristic polynomial on the covariant rational l-adic Tate module for every l different from char(k). Its trace and determinant are independent of l. It is monic of degree twice dim(D), and integral for an integral endomorphism. This is Milne, Abelian Varieties (1986), printed125, Proposition12.9 and its preceding definition.

## Voight chart convention

In the upper-half-plane chart with fixed real embedding i_infinity, the lattice is i_infinity(O)(z,1)^t and the labeled order action uses that embedding. The rational normalizer quotient acts on the maximal-level curve. These are Voight, Quaternion Algebras (2021),43.1.2-43.1.3. The scalar intertwining and degree calculation are separate local calculations.
