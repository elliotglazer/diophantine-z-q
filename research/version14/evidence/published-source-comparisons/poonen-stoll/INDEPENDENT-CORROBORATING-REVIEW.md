For the actual group M=(Q2/Z2)^t direct sum F with F a finite 2-group, the displayed divisible summand is the maximal divisible subgroup. A divisible subgroup maps to a divisible subgroup in F, which must vanish. Conversely the displayed summand is divisible. Also M[2] is finite, M[p]=0 for odd primes, and the intersection of all nM is that same summand. Thus both definitions give the same quotient F here. The author correction does not change the consumed object.

Final Corollary 4 at page1120 identifies the obstruction with Pic^(g−1). In genus one this is Pic^0, which has the rational identity. Corollary 7 and Theorem8 at page1122 therefore give the alternating, nondegenerate pairing on the quotient and its square-order 2-torsion conclusion. The current lemma uses precisely that quotient. It does not infer that the restricted pairing on F[2] is nondegenerate.

The current manuscript b8421edc569a832c9c4271e3592b10df58e75d25f4dc5619c2b4a6766157044d, lines1157–1170, has the finite Kummer equality r+dim Sha[2]=1. Writing Sha[2-infinity]=(Q2/Z2)^t direct sum F gives r+t+dim F[2]=1. The nonnegative last dimension is even by the pairing theorem, hence it is zero. A nontrivial finite 2-group has nonzero 2-torsion, so F=0 and r+t=1. The infinite Selmer corank is r+t. This argument assumes no finiteness of the original Sha.
