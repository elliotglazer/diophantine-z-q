# Desjardins local twist input

## Published statement
Julie Desjardins, Root number of twists of an elliptic curve, Journal de Theorie des Nombres de Bordeaux 32 (2020), 73–101, Proposition 3.3(2), printed page84. For p>=5 and nonzero squarefree integer t, put W_p(E)=D_p W_p(E_t). If p divides t and E has type I0, then D_p=(-1/p). Good reduction has W_p(E)=1 (Proposition3.1, printed83), hence W_p(E_t)=(-1/p). Here E_t is y²=x³+a t² x+b t³ for E:y²=x³+a x+b.

## Product and local conventions
At S, equal squareclasses give isomorphic local twists, so their root numbers agree even for ramified dyadic characters. The parameters are odd signed squarefree integers and units at S. For n=|t|, the product over its prime divisors of (-1/p) is the mod4 character (-1)^((n-1)/2). Since t is coprime to S, these are exactly its newly ramified primes; the sign of t and its fixed dyadic unit class determine n modulo4. Untwisted good primes contribute1, and the real sign is fixed. A parameter congruent3 modulo4 has fundamental discriminant4t, which does not permit substitution into an odd-discriminant-only formula.
