This adds the scope requested by root after SOURCE-FIRST.md had been submitted for its immutable write. I directly extracted Deligne–Serre printed508–511 and visually inspected printed509–510 (PDF4–5). At1.6 the q-expansion formula for T_l is given for l not dividing the level. At1.7 a primitive cuspidal form of type(k,epsilon) has first coefficient1 and is an eigenvector for the Hecke operators with its coefficients as eigenvalues. At1.9 any form of type(k,epsilon) has its Eisenstein-plus-oldform decomposition into scalar multiples of primitive f_i(d_i z), with N_i dividing N, epsilon factoring modulo N_i, and d_i dividing N/N_i; the stated decomposition is unique. These statements are not restricted to weight2. The surrounding discussion explicitly includes weight1; no Li weight-two result is needed for this preliminary choice.

Independent check of the connection: start with the nonzero cuspidal simultaneous good-prime eigenform supplied by the modularity statement, of positive classical weight k and type(k,epsilon). Its decomposition has a nonzero cuspidal primitive constituent. For l not dividing N, l is coprime to every d_i, and the formula1.6 gives T_l(f_i(d_i z)) = (T_l f_i)(d_i z). The primitive form f_i is itself an eigenvector. Uniqueness/linear independence of the decomposition therefore forces a_l(f_i) to equal the original eigenvalue for every l not dividing N whenever the coefficient of f_i(d_i z) is nonzero. Choose such an f_i once; it works for all good primes, has the same k and compatible epsilon, and is normalized by1.7. No assumption that the original eigenform's first Fourier coefficient is nonzero is made.

Matching the corresponding semisimple Galois realization uses the unchanged good-prime characteristic polynomials and Deligne–Serre3.2 (printed513), already read and visually checked. In the k=1 branch, the finite-image realization remains the one in4.1/6.5; in the k>=2 branch, the all-level Kato realization and its dual apply to the selected primitive form. Thus normalization is available before, and independently of, the weight comparison. Later Li use for finite twisting after k=2 is a separate input.

The standard decomposition assertion is consumed as published in1.9, including its cuspidal part and uniqueness; I do not recursively reprove newform theory. The modularity theorem's initial existence of a nonzero classical cuspidal good-prime eigenform remains the existing incoming modularity interface.
