# GTZ limit equality and evaluation

Project paraphrase of Definitions A.2 and A.3, printed p.1324. The local consumer derivation remains in the frozen proof.

**statement**: Ultralimit objects are equivalent when their representatives agree on an ultrafilter-large set; a limit function evaluates by f(lim x_n)=lim f_n(x_n).

**objects**: Sequences of standard objects, spaces and functions, their ultraproducts, and equality of their classes.

**hypotheses**: The functions and points are represented on the stated domains for ultrafilter-almost all indices.

**quantifiers**: Every represented internal function and every represented point of its ultraproduct domain.

**parameter_range**: The fixed nonprincipal ultrafilter framework of Appendix A.

**uniformity**: The evaluation rule holds at all limit points, including nonstandard ones; it does not upgrade a standard-point-only identity.

**constants**: No analytic estimate is part of this rule.

**normalizations**: Equality is exact ultrafilter equality, not equality up to an infinitesimal or standard part.

**exceptional_cases**: External functions need not have representatives. The rule alone supplies neither global equivariance nor a bounded-complexity family.

**limit_order**: Choose representatives of the internal objects, then evaluate and compare their equivalence classes.
