# Four algebraic inputs to the fixed-type CJ comparison

This author attachment supplies the local algebra connections only. It does not supply the geometric class-set realization, genus-character formula, Schwartz transformations or caller support hypotheses.

## Rational embedding
Published Voight14.6.7 says that a separable quadratic field embeds in a quaternion algebra over a global field exactly when it remains a field at every ramified place.
Here B/Q is ramified at q and infinity; F is imaginary and q is inert in F. Thus both required local algebras are fields, giving the rational embedding used at CJproof33. This does not choose an optimal integral embedding or realize any geometric target pair; those are separate steps.

## Specified conjugacy
Published Voight Corollary7.1.5, p97, implements any specified isomorphism of quadratic subfields of a quaternion algebra by inner conjugation. The ground field may be Q or Q_q in these characteristic-zero uses.
Apply it to the two labelled embeddings of the same F. This conjugates the actual homomorphisms, rather than merely their unlabelled images. It is exactly the rational conjugacy at CJproof41. Locally it lets the unramified quadratic field in the division-algebra model be identified with the given embedded F_q. The official August5,2026 erratum corrects the unit-group notation after separable scalar extension and deletes the optional splitting-field parenthesis; neither uncorrected step is used.

## Pointwise centralizer
Published Voight Proposition7.7.8(b), p110, gives dim_k(B)=dim_k(A)*dim_k(C_B(A)) for a simple subalgebra A of a central simple k-algebra B.
Take k=Q and A=F. The dimensions are4 and2, so the centralizer has dimension2. It contains F, hence equals F. Its invertible elements are therefore F-times. This is the pointwise stabilizer in CJproof41–44; the larger normalizer, which can exchange the two embeddings, is not substituted. Corollary7.7.11 is not needed.

## Local division algebra and the two residue orientations
Published Voight13.3.1–4 and Theorem13.3.11, pp205–207, describe the unique division quaternion algebra over a nonarchimedean local field k. With unramified quadratic K/k and uniformizer pi, it has cyclic model K+K*Pi, Pi*a=bar(a)*Pi, Pi^2=pi, valuation w=v(nrd)/2, maximal order S+S*Pi, radical generated by Pi, radical square pi times the order, and residue field the quadratic extension of the base residue field.
Use k=Q_q and the given unramified F_q, identified by specified conjugacy. Every invertible element has the form u*Pi^m for a unit u and integer m: divide by the appropriate Pi-power to make its valuation zero. Units act trivially on the residue field because it is commutative; Pi acts by its nontrivial automorphism. Even m gives unit conjugacy since Pi^2 is central, and odd m gives the opposite residue embedding. By specified conjugacy every labelled embedding is obtained this way. Hence exactly two unit-conjugacy classes occur, distinguished by residue action or by reduced-norm valuation parity, as asserted in CJproof39. Their labels are not identified.
The printed proof has some intermediate valuation expressions without the factor1/2; this attachment uses the defining13.3.1 normalization w=v(nrd)/2 throughout, and only the stated classification and order conclusions. The published correction before13.3.11 recalls cyclic-algebra notation6.1.5. No H19-specific prime3 marking, degree72 or full-endomorphism hypothesis is imported.

## Scope of the remaining joins
Ribet geometry supplies the actual enhanced target objects; CM and good reduction supply the source and its geometric reduction. The four inputs above permit the algebraic comparison of those objects. They do not prove analytic orientation transformations, finite-character formulas, or the arithmetic support conditions at an arbitrary caller.
