# The actual geometric Atkin–Lehner operation

This note supplies the global geometric identification consumed at cj-conductor-one-v1/proof.md line66 and main15876–15886. It is an author proposal, not an independent approval. The analytic calculation was suggested by /root/v14_sign_conductor and checked here by /root/v14_h6_coverage. The actual CM model, labelled O-action, cyclic level subgroup and good finite diagram are incoming data.

## 1. The quotient and its new level subgroup

Write N=n m with n=p^e exactly dividing N, so gcd(n,m)=1. For a characteristic-zero elliptic curve E with cyclic subgroup C_N of order N, write C_n and C_m for its primary factors. Let phi:E→E'=E/C_n be the quotient. Define the new level subgroup as the sum of phi(E[n]) and phi(C_m). The first is ker(phi-dual): both dual composites are[n], and phi(E[n]) has order n, equal to the degree of the separable dual. The second has order m, since phi has kernel of order n prime to m. Thus their sum is cyclic of order N. This uses the general dual-isogeny identities, not a special CM raising formula.

## 2. Identification with the published matrix

Use E_z=C/(Z z+Z) and C_N generated by1/N. Atkin–Lehner's exact-divisor matrix is W_n=[[n a,b],[N c,n d]], with n a d-m b c=1. Put z'=(n a z+b)/(N c z+n d) and s=n/(N c z+n d). Direct multiplication gives

    s z = n d z' - b,
    s   = -N c z' + n a.

The lattice inclusion s(Z z+Z)⊂Z z'+Z has determinant n. Also s/n=a-m c z' lies in the target lattice. Hence multiplication by s induces a degree-n isogeny with kernel precisely C_n. Its image of E[n] is generated by d z'-b/n and a-m c z', so is the subgroup generated by1/n: b is a unit modulo n because n a d-m b c=1. The image of C_m is generated by n a/m-n c z', so is the subgroup generated by1/m: n a is a unit modulo m. Their sum is generated by1/N. Therefore the quotient construction in section1 gives exactly the published W_n action on the pair (E_z,C_N), not its inverse or an unnormalized matrix action.

Every complex elliptic curve with a cyclic subgroup has such a presentation. The quotient construction is algebraic and natural under isomorphism and field extension. The incoming modular interpretation therefore identifies this action with the actual Atkin–Lehner operation on the noncuspidal moduli points used here. Extension to the cusps and the differential eigen-action are separate retained inputs; neither is needed to manufacture these geometric anchors.

## 3. The labelled split O-action

In the present conductor-one setting O is maximal and p splits in its field. The cyclic C_n is one O-stable eigensummand of E[n]. On a split p-adic lattice write Lambda=Lambda_1⊕Lambda_2, with C_n=(p^(-e)Lambda_1)/Lambda_1. The quotient lattice is Lambda'=p^(-e)Lambda_1⊕Lambda_2. Inside E'=V/Lambda', the image phi(E[n]) is p^(-e)Lambda_2/Lambda_2. Thus the new distinguished n-part is the complementary O-eigensummand. This is an exchange of integral summands/projectors, so it works at p=2 as well; no division by two or distinction solely by residue eigenvalues is used.

For every other prime dividing m the isogeny is an O-linear isomorphism on the corresponding level torsion and carries its chosen subgroup to phi(C_m). Hence those labelled choices are unchanged. In the supplied split Eichler model, exchanging precisely these two lattice-chain endpoints is the nontrivial local normalizer coset represented by [[0,1],[-p^e,0]]. Changing adapted bases contributes level-preserving units only. At other level primes the operation is the identity on the labelled local moduli data under phi, not a claim that the rational matrix W_n literally has identity entries there. This is the geometric interpretation required for the accepted local projector computation.

## 4. Reduction in the actual finite diagram

Here p divides N and q=ell_0 does not divide N. Thus n is prime to q. Work over the supplied common good local model at the fixed reduction place, enlarged finitely if necessary to realize the finite diagram. Its n-torsion and the subgroup C_n extend as finite etale groups. The retained quotient and mapping-property inputs extend phi and its dual. Their identities extend from the generic fibre; the kernel image phi(E[n]) and the transported other level subgroups specialize compatibly. Consequently the reduced operation is the same degree-n quotient with the same dual subgroup. Its differential at q is nonzero and O-linear, so it preserves the chosen tangent residue embedding, rather than conjugating the q-orientation. Repeating at finitely many p dividing N supplies every level-orientation tuple at that same q-orientation.

The common good model, finite-etale torsion, extension/quotient compatibility and modular interpretation are explicit prerequisites already used by the conductor-one proof. The argument does not derive them from the normalizer matrix. It does not identify the opposite q-orientation with the image of the same CM-type reduction map; the separate Frobenius operation handles that orientation.
