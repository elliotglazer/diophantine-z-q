## Independent reconstruction

For an invertible L on X, choose opens of A on whose inverse images L is trivial. If u is a transition unit, the transition function for det(f_*L) tensor det(f_*O_X)^(-1) is det(m_u), where m_u is multiplication by u on f_*O_X. This is the function norm. Since m_uv=m_u m_v, the construction is multiplicative in L. Finite pushforward on modules and determinants commute with arbitrary base change. Thus the determinant expression is the genuine functorial line-bundle norm.

For each Q-scheme T, twisting L by pr_X^*M changes its norm by pr_A^*(M^tensor d). Consequently the norm descends to families modulo line bundles from T and then to relative Picard sheaves. On geometric fibres it preserves degree by divisor pushforward, so it restricts to the degree-zero sheaves. Representability plus Yoneda gives a Q-group scheme morphism J_1(N)->Pic^0(A). This explains the algebraic morphism, not just its effect on points.

The family O(Delta - {O} times A) defines an algebraic Abel map A->Pic^0(A). The elliptic divisor-class theorem gives its geometric point bijection and compatibility with addition. In characteristic zero this bijection of smooth projective curves has degree one and is an isomorphism. This supplies the algebraic content of Pic^0(A)=A.

Over Qbar, fix any P0 in X_1(N). Given a in A(Qbar), choose P with f(P)=a+f(P0). Then pushforward sends [P]-[P0] to [a+f(P0)]-[f(P0)], which corresponds to a in A. The image of the morphism is closed because its domain is proper; containing all geometric points proves surjectivity. No Q-rational P0 or rational preimage is used. No degree factor occurs, since f_*[P]=[f(P)] over Qbar. The separate identity Norm(f^*L)=L^tensor d concerns a different composite.
