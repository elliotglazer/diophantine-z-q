# Selected published inputs

These are project-authored summaries of the pinned published inputs. They are not new proofs or a claim of full-source comparison. The separate application proof derives its outputs from these inputs and its other stated premises.

## MILNE-LOCAL
Local reciprocity identifies finite characters with characters of completed K^*, and Br(K) has invariant Q/Z for nonarchimedean local K.
Source: 2c6195ec76a974f3f336c77cb71cc3845b018aad2d43136716b3477a3bc5fb31; I.1.6(b),p19; I.2 introduction,p26.
Version and scope: Second edition2006 publisher-production text; original addendum retained. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## MILNE-BRAUER
For a global field, Br(K) injects into the sum of all local Brauer groups, and the invariant sum has kernel equal to that image.
Source: 2c6195ec76a974f3f336c77cb71cc3845b018aad2d43136716b3477a3bc5fb31; Proof of II.2.1,p164.
Version and scope: Same2006 edition. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## LI
The determinant-normalized Fricke operator sends a normalized newform of arbitrary character to a nonzero scalar times its coefficient conjugate; primitive sources and their level are determined by the cited newform results.
Source: 07530575e712b4ded14ce64f877ca4f9383bbc7a020cb7dbd70fe88e6ab3ccb2; Printed285 slash;294 primitive decomposition;296 paragraph beforeLemma6;298 Theorem5.
Version and scope: Original published1975 page scans; companion pages separately pinned. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## SHIMURA
A normalized common weight-two Hecke eigenform for Gamma1(N)⊂Gamma⊂Gamma0(N) has the Q-quotient, coefficient action, dimension and conjugate differential span of Theorem1; the product Euler formula requires Gamma0 or the stated Fricke stability.
Source: 6a34cc46d06c0f5d4e7d380ddcbc45ec3143b19359bfebba5b1c8f00976f3308; Definition of group of levelN,p523; Theorem1 and proof,pp526–527.
Version and scope: Original published JMSJ25(1973)523–544. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.
The unconditional clause selected for C4 gives the coefficient-conjugate normalized eigenforms at the same level. C4 does not apply the conditional Euler-product clause.

## EMERTON-CLASSICAL
Every cuspidal newform has the stated irreducible cohomological representation and good geometric-Frobenius polynomial; at weight2 the polynomial is X^2-a_l X+chi(l)l.
Source: 5b2042b8863162f06ba822c4e463bf4290163ad2b57baca05f5ecb71693243fb; Section2.3,p292, equation4.
Version and scope: Final PAMQ2(2006)279–393, classical attachment only. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## FALTINGS
The rational Tate module of an abelian variety over a number field is semisimple.
Source: 738d6b5f716e05b8180066f665e52170576061c1ba75802d1e4f3c4cc1d69614; Section5 Satz3,p360, retaining1984erratum p381.
Version and scope: Original published1983 article plus original published1984 correction. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## SERRE-TRACE
Chebotarev gives density after finite exclusions; over any characteristic-zero field, finite-dimensional semisimple representations of an algebra with equal trace are isomorphic.
Source: 971dbfdd4414d99ea21d7d0af63dbb790d7b1188e9aec37f422181a2b543dec4; I.2.2,ppI-7–I-8; standalone lemma I.2.3,pI-11.
Version and scope: Published book; surrounding Q-rational compatible-system theorem is not used. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.

## MILNE-H1
H1_et(A,Z_l) is canonically Hom(T_l A,Z_l), compatible with the natural Galois action for an abelian variety over a base field, l different from its characteristic.
Source: 86259344a576e4ef75a9d81e9bf43143f872a72b14cf8fcc2703f3aea6349136; Theorem15.1(a),p129; Remark15.5,p131.
Version and scope: Original Arithmetic Geometry(1986) chapter, not the later course-note numbering. Published theorem input; no recursive reproof or newly broadened edition comparison claimed.
