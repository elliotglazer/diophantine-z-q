BM author p17 defines the smooth category with Z acting by zeta, and defines C(O) by Pontryagin duality. On the dual, `(z f)(v)=f(z^-1 v)=zeta(z)^-1 f(v)`. Thus the compact quotient algebra must use eta=zeta_BM^-1. The new sentence states this correctly and distinguishes it from the smooth-side zeta in Section 1. BM p29 dualizes the fixed-center K-injective envelope into exactly this compact category.

For the second clarification, BM p29 identifies the reduction of Coker(x) with the projective envelope of the finite K-socle dual. Choose finitely many generators of this finite target and lifts to its projective cover Pbar. The resulting continuous map from a finite free compact R/pi-module has compact, hence closed, image. That image surjects onto the target. Essentiality of the cover forces the image to be Pbar. Since Pbar is projective, the surjection splits continuously. Therefore Pbar is a direct summand of a finite free module and is finite projective. This supplies the actual finiteness hypothesis of the new lifting lemma; mere unspecified projectivity would not have supplied it.

The statements do not assert existence of the preceding G-compatible injective enlargement or of x. They use the exact reduction and torsion-freeness already established by the displayed source argument. This acceptance is limited to these two added connections and preserves the source-version qualifications of the main reviews. No source or author record was modified.
