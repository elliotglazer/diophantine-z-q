## Published input

For rational coefficient field this is the single line generated by f(z)dz.

## Actual fixed quotient

The current construction takes the rational coefficient eigenform f, q_f:J0(N)->A_f and a Q-isogeny alpha:A_f->E, then phi=alpha q_f and pi(x)=phi([x]-[infinity]). Pullback by the characteristic-zero isogeny alpha is a nonzero isomorphism on the one-dimensional invariant differential spaces. Thus for a nonzero invariant differential omega on E, pi*omega=c f(z)dz for a nonzero scalar c. No value or integrality assertion about c is needed.

## Conditional involution calculation

Let W be the actual Atkin-Lehner involution in main3827, with the explicitly stipulated action W*(f(z)dz)=w f(z)dz. Since W squared is identity and f is nonzero, w is1 or-1. The difference morphism h=pi composed with W minus [w]pi has pullback h*omega=W*pi*omega-w pi*omega=0. Here invariance of omega gives the addition and negation formulas.

On a complex local chart of E, omega is nonvanishing, so h*omega=0 says every local coordinate of h has derivative zero. Hence h is locally constant and is constant on the connected modular curve. Evaluating at infinity and using pi(infinity)=0 gives pi(Wx)=[w]pi(x)+pi(W infinity). Equality over the complex numbers is equality of the original algebraic morphisms after base change.
