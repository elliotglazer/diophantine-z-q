## A055 rational connection
For rational s>0 write 1/s=a/b with positive integers a,b. Choose integers u_i with sum u_i^2=ab and put z_i=u_i/b. Then s sum z_i^2=1. Conversely this equation implies sum z_i^2>0 and s>0 in Q. Every nonnegative rational a is a sum of four rational squares by the same denominator clearing (zero uses four zeroes). Thus a=sum z_i^2 and a^2=x^2 holds precisely for a=|x|. These assertions concern rational witnesses and do not assert their integrality. They supply current lines258–269.
