# GT-GOWERS-CS-NORMALIZATION — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green and Terence Tao, Linear equations in primes, Annals of Mathematics 171 (2010), 1753–1850. Final publisher PDF SHA256 ec126f9c9e189cc0308b83fb4f9f93c29a47d11ad875391aff4ce4c42aef1001. Locator: Appendix B, equations (B.12), (B.13) and (B.14), p.1817.

**statement**: The absolute value of a finite abelian-group cube average is at most the product of the Gowers norms of its vertex functions. For a nonempty subset A, the localized Gowers norm equals the norm of the zero extension divided by the norm of 1_A.

**objects**: A finite additive group Z, complex vertex functions, a nonempty A subset Z and zero extensions.

**hypotheses**: The cube dimension and normalized uniform expectations of Appendix B; use the alternating conjugations in the cube form.

**quantifiers**: Every collection of vertex functions and every nonempty subset in the stated finite group.

**parameter range**: The current application has cube dimension d+1>=2 and a cyclic ambient group of size comparable to N.

**uniformity**: Exact inequalities and normalization identities independent of N and the vertex functions.

**constants**: Constant one in the group Gowers–Cauchy–Schwarz inequality; the denominator norm of 1_A must be retained.

**normalizations**: Normalized uniform averages, zero extension, and the precise local/global norm ratio.

**exceptional cases**: No automatic replacement of a weighted integer cube average by a cyclic one is asserted; no cutoff Fourier coefficient is discarded.

**limit order**: The local proof establishes no-wrap embedding and summable Fourier weights before applying the group inequality, then converts back with B.14.

**Application boundary**: The consuming proof supplies the smooth averaging, phase absorption, finite coefficient sum and interval normalization bounds. These do not follow from B.12 alone.
