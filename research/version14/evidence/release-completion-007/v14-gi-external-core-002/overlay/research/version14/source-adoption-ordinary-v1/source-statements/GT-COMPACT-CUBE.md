# GT-COMPACT-CUBE — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green and Terence Tao, Linear equations in primes, Annals of Mathematics 171 (2010), 1753–1850. Final publisher PDF SHA256 ec126f9c9e189cc0308b83fb4f9f93c29a47d11ad875391aff4ce4c42aef1001. Locator: Definition 11.4 and Proposition 11.5, p.1799; proof in Appendix E.

**statement**: On a fixed s-step nilmanifold the zero vertex of every linear-orbit (s+1)-cube is a continuous function of the remaining vertices on one fixed compact subset of their product space.

**objects**: M=G/Gamma, Sigma contained in M to the power {0,1}^{s+1} minus zero, and a continuous P:Sigma to M.

**hypotheses**: The fixed s-step nilmanifold hypotheses and the linear-orbit parallelepiped definition x_omega=g^{n+omega dot h}x.

**quantifiers**: Every g in G, x in M, n in Z and h in Z^{s+1}.

**parameter range**: Fixed s>=1 and fixed nilmanifold.

**uniformity**: Sigma and P are independent of g,x,n,h and interval length.

**constants**: A topological conclusion, without a quantitative modulus uniform over changing nilmanifolds.

**normalizations**: All 2^{s+1}-1 nonzero vertices are retained.

**exceptional cases**: The source concerns linear orbits; polynomial sequences first require the separately proved same-step linearization.

**limit order**: Fix the nilmanifold and its cube constraint, then approximate composed test functions in the local proof.

**Application boundary**: The local converse does not import Proposition 11.2’s averaging-domain transition. The compact tensor approximation, normalized smooth averaging and Fourier cutoff estimates are separate local arguments.
