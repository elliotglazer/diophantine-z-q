# GTZ-INITIAL-SELECTION — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green, Terence Tao and Tamar Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm, Annals of Mathematics 176 (2012), 1231–1372. Final publisher PDF SHA256 cd320f5c1217df9bb41395e847ae2a924e70521021ca1b01c7abc20550123be1. Locator: Lemma 6.4 and Corollary 6.5, pp.1255–1256.

**statement**: A scalar degree-d nilsequence is uniformly approximable by a bounded linear combination of components of degree-d unit-vector nilcharacters. Nonzero degree-d bias is consequently witnessed by correlation with a degree-d nilcharacter.

**objects**: Scalar nilsequences, vector nilcharacters and their scalar components in the conventions of Section 6.

**hypotheses**: The degree-d nilsequence and bias hypotheses; the proof uses global ordinary vertical tests and the conjugation-stable point-separating algebra they generate.

**quantifiers**: Each fixed nilsequence and positive approximation tolerance; each biased sequence under the stated boundedness assumptions.

**parameter range**: Fixed standard degree d; the current initial Section 7 use has d=s-1>0.

**uniformity**: The source gives a finite approximation for a fixed test; parameter-uniform finite witness lists are established in the local compact-net argument.

**constants**: Finite coefficient and geometry bounds must be retained when passing from approximation to positive component correlation.

**normalizations**: Correlation uses a selected scalar component of a globally unit vector; the vector is retained for subsequent symbol operations.

**exceptional cases**: No claim that every weak object already satisfies the project strong convention; no prescribed-symbol selection is supplied.

**limit order**: Construct ordinary approximants, take a finite net for the fixed bounded class, and only then choose a witness internally in the consuming proof.

**Application boundary**: The reviewed assembly initial-selection construction supplies the last two steps and the actual strong represented family. This card imports the published approximation and bias criterion only.
