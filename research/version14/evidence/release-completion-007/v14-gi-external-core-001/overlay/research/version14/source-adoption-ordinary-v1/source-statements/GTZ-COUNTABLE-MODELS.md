# GTZ-COUNTABLE-MODELS — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green, Terence Tao and Tamar Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm, Annals of Mathematics 176 (2012), 1231–1372. Final publisher PDF SHA256 cd320f5c1217df9bb41395e847ae2a924e70521021ca1b01c7abc20550123be1. Locator: Section 6, p.1250, with the Malcev-coordinate framework of Section 3.

**statement**: At fixed finite degree the rational filtered nilmanifolds in the stated Malcev framework have countably many isomorphism classes; one may choose a smooth compact metric on each representative, and metrics on a fixed compact smooth model are equivalent.

**objects**: Rational filtered lattice models and fixed smooth metrics on their compact quotients.

**hypotheses**: The rational finite-dimensional filtered models of the paper; metric comparison takes place on one fixed compact model.

**quantifiers**: All models in the stated rational class, and every pair of fixed smooth metrics on one model.

**parameter range**: Fixed finite degree; countably many finite dimensions and rational structure data.

**uniformity**: Countability enables an enumeration; it does not give a common geometric constant for every model.

**constants**: Metric equivalence constants depend on the fixed model and metrics.

**normalizations**: Choose representatives up to filtered lattice isomorphism before enumerating bounded Lipschitz tests.

**exceptional cases**: No finite list of all rational models and no global metric-equivalence bound is asserted.

**limit order**: Enumerate models, choose their fixed metrics, then use the local finite-family contradiction argument.

**Application boundary**: The local assembly proof supplies the diagonal finite-family selection. Countability is a qualitative source premise, not a uniform bound on an unrestricted family.
