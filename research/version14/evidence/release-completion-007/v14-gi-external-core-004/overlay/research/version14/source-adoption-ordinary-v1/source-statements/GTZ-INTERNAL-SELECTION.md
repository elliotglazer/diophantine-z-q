# GTZ internal selection

This is a project paraphrase of Definition A.10 and Lemma A.11, GTZ final pp.1330; pointwise boundedness A.5 is on p.1327. Final PDF SHA256 cd320f5c1217df9bb41395e847ae2a924e70521021ca1b01c7abc20550123be1.

**statement**: An internal relation R between internal sets X and Y with a witness in a sigma-limit subset S of Y for every x admits an internal witness selection into S. Its increasing-stage proof bounds the selected stage by a standard integer.

**objects**: Internal X,Y,R and S equal to the union over standard indices of an internal increasing exhaustion.

**hypotheses**: Every x has a witness in S; the exhaustion is one internal sequence as required by Definition A.10.

**quantifiers**: Every x in X, selected simultaneously by one internal map.

**parameter_range**: The natural ultrapower framework of Appendix A; stages indexed by limit natural numbers.

**uniformity**: One internal witness map; the stage can be bounded by a standard integer when the least-stage map is pointwise standard-bounded.

**constants**: No effective value of the standard bound is asserted.

**normalizations**: An external positive-standard condition must be encoded by an internal reciprocal-threshold coordinate.

**exceptional_cases**: An arbitrary countable family of internal sets is not automatically the specified sigma-limit presentation. The local finite-intersection proof avoids an unjustified diagonal representative.

**limit_order**: Construct the exhaustion and internal relation, prove pointwise standard-stage existence, then select.

The local later-selection derivation supplies an explicit finite-intersection representative proof for bounded stages. The ambiguous A.12 tagged-set display and any unjustified x_(i,i) diagonal choice are not used.
