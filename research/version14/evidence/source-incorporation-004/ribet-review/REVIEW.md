## The available geometric result and its actual application

Ribet Section 3 begins with distinct auxiliary primes p and q and an arbitrary positive M prime to pq. The application sets M=N and q=ell_0 and chooses an unused p. The geometric statements impose no residual-representation condition at this unused prime. The arbitrary exponent n=v_l(M)>0 in the proof on p442 confirms that squarefreeness is not required, including at two.

Page439 describes enhanced geometric elliptic curves and their Eichler endomorphism orders. Proposition3.3 and Remark3.5(a) give the class-set and right-Hom-module descriptions. Proposition3.6 allows the initially chosen Eichler order as reference. Remark3.5(b) supplies the Frobenius involution and its right-ideal operation. These are the particular published inputs used here, rather than the modularity theorem in the article's introduction.

For A052-RIBET-GEOMETRY, I checked the coordinate directly. With a rational quasi-isogeny lambda from the enhanced curve to the reference curve, the module Hom(E0,E) embeds by phi mapping to lambda phi. It is a right R-module. A completed generator gives I_hat=z R_hat. Changing the rational identification acts on the left, and changing the integral generator acts on the right. Endomorphisms act on this module on the left, so their order is B intersect z R_hat z^-1. Its units give the required automorphism group. This agrees with the order formula on p442. It neither assumes that all Eichler orders are globally conjugate nor divides the automorphism group by its central signs.

The transporter printed on p440 must not be copied into this calculation without fixing its direction. The candidate explicitly selects the right-Hom convention of Remark3.5(a). The module calculation fixes z independently of any opposite-direction transporter notation. This is material because reversing z without also changing the left-order formula would change the application.

For A052-RIBET-TATE, the actual ideal-isogeny construction remains a separate premise. Given that construction, a prime-to-Nq ideal replaces each prime-to-q CM lattice by a^-1 times that lattice in the same rational identification. This gives a^-1 z in the fixed convention. At q the prime-to-q isogeny is an isomorphism on the q-divisible group, and at primes of N it preserves the chosen level eigensummand. The source's q component is its specified Dieudonne-module convention, not an etale rank-two q-adic Tate module. The connection does not infer existence or specialization of the ideal quotient merely from the class-set bijection.

For A052-RIBET-FROBENIUS, Remark3.5(b) explicitly supplies the operation used by the manuscript. In the right-ideal description the distinguished subideal has index q^2. Locally this is multiplication by a division uniformizer on the right; at the other primes the operation is the identity. This is consistent with the manuscript's norm valuation one and with its separate conjugation calculation on the quaternion residue field. The operation on the underlying coarse point does not identify the two labelled residue embeddings.

## Coarse rationality and the actual finite-sum consumer

For A052-COARSE-DESCENT, let x be a supersingular geometric point of X0(N) over F_q. Its coordinates are algebraic over F_q, so its closed-point residue field is F_(q^d) for some finite d. The orbit of a geometric lift under q-Frobenius has length d. The supplied involution implies d divides two. Thus the point is F_(q^2)-valued. This argument concerns a point of the coarse scheme and makes no assertion that all labelled moduli objects descend.

The reduced parametrization is over F_q by a retained good-reduction input. It therefore sends this point to E(F_(q^2)). That is exactly the domain of the previously constructed lambda_M, whose definition and Frobenius sign I read at base lines14125--14154. Arithmetic and geometric q-Frobenius are inverses, and agree on these F_(q^2) points and their images. Consequently the same lambda_M evaluates the finite sum and the orientation comparison. No scalar-Frobenius model of each supersingular elliptic curve is required.

I read base lines14170--14186,14970--15064 and the relevant conductor-one proof sections.
