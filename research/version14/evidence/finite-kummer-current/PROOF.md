# Finite radical pairing

This is a new local proof for the actual multiplicative Kummer input. It replaces the online-note theorem supplier; it is not a quotation from a published source.

## Statement

Let F be a field of characteristic prime to n containing all n-th roots of unity, with n>=1. Let B be a finite subgroup of F*/F*^n, and choose finitely many representatives a_i whose classes generate B. In a fixed algebraic closure choose r_i with r_i^n=a_i and put L=F(r_i). Then L/F is finite Galois and the pairing Gal(L/F) x B -> mu_n, given by (g,[a]) -> g(r)/r for r^n=a in L, is well-defined and perfect. Thus every homomorphism B -> mu_n is realized by one automorphism of L/F, and [L:F]=|B|.

## Proof

Each polynomial X^n-a_i is separable, and all its roots are zeta r_i with zeta in mu_n contained in F. Hence L is its finite splitting field and L/F is finite Galois. The map g -> (g(r_i)/r_i)_i embeds its Galois group G into the finite abelian group mu_n^s. In particular G is abelian of exponent dividing n.

Every class in B has a representative a=c^n product_i a_i^(e_i), and r=c product_i r_i^(e_i) lies in L with r^n=a. Changing the root multiplies it by an element of mu_n in F. Changing a by an n-th power in F multiplies a root by an element of F. Thus g(r)/r is independent of both choices. Multiplying roots proves multiplicativity in B. Since each g fixes mu_n, composition proves multiplicativity in G.

If an element g pairs trivially with all B, it fixes every r_i and therefore fixes L. If a class b pairs trivially with all G, choose its root r in L as above. Every element of G fixes r, so the fixed-field theorem gives r in F. Therefore b is trivial in F*/F*^n. The pairing is nondegenerate in both variables.

For any finite abelian group A of exponent dividing n, write A as a product of finite cyclic groups of orders dividing n. Each cyclic factor of order d has exactly d homomorphisms to mu_n, because mu_n contains exactly d roots of unity killed by d. Therefore |Hom(A,mu_n)|=|A|. Nondegeneracy gives injections G -> Hom(B,mu_n) and B -> Hom(G,mu_n). The two cardinal inequalities force equality, so both injections are isomorphisms. This proves the statement.

## Actual use and limits

In main4429–4447 take F=K(mu_(2^infinity)) and n=2^m at the actual finite stage. B is generated by the finitely many stated radicands modulo F*^n. The preceding valuation argument in the manuscript checks that the prescribed multipliers respect every relation in B. They therefore define a homomorphism B -> mu_n, and the proved pairing gives the required automorphism. F may be an infinite extension: the proof only uses the finite extension L/F. No finite-layer descent, Chebotarev statement, independence of the radicands or class-number division follows from this lemma.

The proof uses elementary splitting-field Galois theory, its fixed-field theorem, and the structure theorem for finite abelian groups. Those foundational inputs are stated explicitly; no analytic or arithmetic theorem is imported.
