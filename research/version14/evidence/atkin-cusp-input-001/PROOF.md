# Cusp permutation for the actual Atkin-Lehner involution

This proves only cusp permutation and the resulting cuspidal constant. The eigen-action on the fixed differential line remains the explicit hypothesis of main3827.

Use the usual matrix definition of an Atkin-Lehner operator. Write N=e*r with gcd(e,r)=1, and choose integers a,b,c,d with e*a*d-r*b*c=1. Its matrix is M=[[e*a,b],[N*c,e*d]], of determinant e. The case of a product of these operators follows by composition.

For gamma=[[alpha,beta],[N*t,delta]] in Gamma0(N), direct multiplication gives

M gamma M^-1 = [[e*a*d*alpha+b*N*d*t-a*N*c*beta-b*r*c*delta, -a*b*alpha-b*b*r*t+e*a*a*beta+a*b*delta], [N*(c*d*alpha+e*d*d*t-r*c*c*beta-c*d*delta), -r*b*c*alpha-b*d*N*t+a*N*c*beta+e*a*d*delta]].

Every entry is integral, the lower-left is divisible by N, and the determinant is1. Thus M normalizes Gamma0(N) in one direction. Moreover M^2/e=[[e*a*a+r*b*c,b*(a+d)],[N*c*(a+d),r*b*c+e*d*d]] belongs to Gamma0(N); its determinant is1. Therefore conjugation in the other direction also preserves Gamma0(N), and the induced operator is the stated involution.

An invertible rational matrix acts bijectively on P1(Q), including infinity, and its inverse is rational. Since M normalizes Gamma0(N), it acts bijectively on the cusp classes Gamma0(N) backslash P1(Q). This is exactly the cusp set in the compactified modular curve. Thus the actual W permutes cusps; no assertion about its eigenvalue on a differential is used.

For pi(x)=phi([x]-[infinity]), it follows that pi(W infinity)=phi([W infinity]-[infinity]) belongs to phi(C_N), where C_N is generated by all cusp differences. The separately supplied Manin-Drinfeld finiteness and chosen annihilator C_pi kill this point. This argument does not prove that finiteness, the differential eigen-action, the based formula, any root-number relation, or an integral-model extension.
