The preceding local construction gives a rational Lie homomorphism d rho in the chosen rational bases. In a simply connected nilpotent group, rho(exp x)=exp(d rho(x)); hence first-kind coordinates of the image are rational linear functions of x. Compose with the rational polynomial conversion to second-kind Malcev coordinates. The resulting finite collection of polynomials P_j is rational and satisfies P_j(0)=0. Write each as a sum of c_alpha x^alpha with |alpha|>=1. If D is a multiple of every denominator of c_alpha, then c_alpha D^|alpha| is an integer, so P_j(Dx) is integral for every integer vector x. Definition2.1 identifies these integer second-kind coordinates with Gamma. Choose D also divisible by the prior BCH denominator requirement. This proves the asserted inclusion and preserves that divisibility requirement.
