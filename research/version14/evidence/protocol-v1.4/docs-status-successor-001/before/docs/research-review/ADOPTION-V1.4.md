# Protocol v1.4 adoption

The project owner supplied and adopted version 1.4 on November 7, 2026. The current `PROTOCOL.md` preserves that supplied document byte for byte. Version 1.3 is retained as `protocol-v1.3.md`; earlier evidence keeps its original versions and review scope.

This adoption adds explicit review of substantive connecting arguments, bounded review of related uses after a failure, and consistency checks for repeated current assertions. It retains the complete published-source, source-first coverage, independent review and cumulative release-handoff requirements. The ongoing GTZ repair and its affected consumers are subject to these requirements.

Policy adoption is effective for current project work. The v1.4 controller implementation, its independent acceptance and deployment are separate work. The existing controller's v1.3 checks do not establish v1.4 conformance. No earlier review is relabeled as a v1.4 review.

Version 14 remains an unreleased candidate. Its open mathematical and source-review obligations remain open; neither this policy change nor a structural check resolves them. Quartic optimization remains paused. Publication to both repositories must follow the existing protected merge and readback process.
