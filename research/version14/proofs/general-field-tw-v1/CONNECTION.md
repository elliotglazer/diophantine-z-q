# Connection to the actual auxiliary-prime consumer

This is an incorporation proposal, not an independent acceptance. The proof
in PROOF.md is exactly theta003 (69d535145031e224f2eec621a243de6d492facc17bf85a5c27dbe8c3a89907fd).

Kisin's final 2009 paper defines the rings in Section 3.2.1, pp.625–626:
the local determinant is psi times the cyclotomic character, psi is totally
even, all S-frames include infinite places, and the ring in Proposition
3.2.2(2) fixes determinant only at S. The fully fixed-determinant global
ring is a different object. PROOF Sections 1 and 5 preserve that distinction.

For the same fixed F, residual representation and S, the proof supplies all
three conclusions of Proposition 3.2.2. Section 4 gives q=h1−2 distinct primes,
the required norm congruence and distinct residual eigenvalues. Section 5
gives the actual locally-fixed/global-variable relative tangent sequence and
2q+1 generators. Section 6 gives the rank t=2−|S|+q character quotient at the
full requested exponent by choosing j>=max(n+2,j0+1). No uniformity in F,
nested prime sets, arbitrary odd-prime extension, or additional local-ring
smoothness is claimed.

These are the quantities actually used in Kisin 3.2.3, p.626, to choose Q_n,
and in 3.2.9, p.628: h=q counts diamond variables, g=2q+1 counts the relative
generators, and t is the rank of the character torus. The explanation on
p.629 explicitly derives patched-ring property (i) from 3.2.2. The rest of
3.2.9 still needs its separate automorphic module, local geometry, support,
torsor, and augmentation premises. The present proof does not supply those
by implication. In particular it does not reprove the M0 correction, the
non-nodal component argument, or automorphic base change.

The main manuscript imports the primitive-GL2 consequence of strong Serre
in its modularity lemma. Its two direct uses return the same original r5;
the same-r3 and conductor/height continuation is unchanged. The new paragraph
identifies this subsidiary two-adic input in that ancestry. It does not
replace ICM Theorem1.4 by Proposition3.2.2 or assert that auxiliary primes
alone prove modularity.
