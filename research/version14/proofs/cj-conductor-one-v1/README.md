# Direct conductor-one reduction argument

This successor preserves the prior unadopted 001 proposal and corrects its historical CJ DOI to 10.1112/blms/bds098. The TeX candidate and mathematical proof are unchanged.

This package preserves the exact authored conductor-one proof and its conditional independent review. The proof supplies the A028 reduction/orientation and ideal-action connection for maximal CM order, split prime-power level and one inert reduction prime. It does not prove the general conductor-changing Cornut–Jetchev theorem.

Read [proof.md](proof.md), [PREMISES.json](PREMISES.json) and [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) together. The proof's original proposal-status sentences are preserved historical bytes; [reviews/conditional-mathematics.md](reviews/conditional-mathematics.md) records the later bounded decision. That decision retains A052, CM reciprocity and good-reduction facts as real dependencies. A052's final-edition comparison and the precise Frobenius/right-division-uniformizer input remain open. No final source paper is included.
