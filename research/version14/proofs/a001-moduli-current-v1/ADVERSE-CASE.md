# Adjacent published proof concern, not a theorem-falsity claim

While reading the final Baba--Granath source for the current A001 application, I inspected Proposition3.7 and its proof, printed743, PDF10. The source defines the two degree-two quotient maps f_i from the genus-two curve C(-24) to its elliptic quotients. It defines phi:J(C)->E_1 x E_2 by the pair of induced pushforward maps. It then infers that phi is an isomorphism from the assertion that f_1(a)=f_1(b) and f_2(a)=f_2(b) together force a=b on the curve.

That last inference does not establish injectivity on the Jacobian. More precisely, for the actual pair of complementary bielliptic quotients the displayed homomorphism has degree4. Let phi^dagger:E_1 x E_2 -> J be the pair of pullback maps, the dual under the canonical principal polarizations. Each f_i,* f_i^* is [2]. The off-diagonal composites vanish: the involution defining one quotient acts as +1 on its pulled-back elliptic part and -1 on the other, since the two involutions differ by the hyperelliptic involution. Thus each off-diagonal homomorphism equals its negative; Hom of abelian varieties has no nonzero torsion, so it is zero. Hence phi phi^dagger=([2],[2]). It has degree16 on the product abelian surface. Dual isogenies have the same degree, so deg(phi)^2=16 and deg(phi)=4.

This identifies an issue with this particular morphism and proof sentence. It does not prove that J is not isomorphic, as an unpolarized abelian variety, to E_1 x E_2 through another map. It also does not by itself disprove the endomorphism-ring statement, the rational invariant formula, or any later theorem.

The narrowed A001 quotient argument uses Theorem2.1, Lemma3.1, the fixed-point table and the unique coordinate with its three assigned CM values. It does not require this morphism to be an isomorphism. However the later normalized weighted-Igusa formula is supplied by final Lin--Yang equation(2), which attributes it to Baba--Granath. Baba--Granath Proposition3.9 uses the CM calculation in Proposition3.7. Therefore this packet must not claim that the whole later invariant formula has no ancestral relation to this concern. An independent disposition or a direct repair of the needed CM/invariant connection remains separate. Isogeny supplies the rational CM field, but the integral order and precise point identification must not be inferred from isogeny alone.
