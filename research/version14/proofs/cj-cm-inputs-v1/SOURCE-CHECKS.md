# Source checks and scope

The primary mathematical sources are final published texts already held in the source archive. The new work is their application to the labelled conductor-one domain, not a claim that a missing inventory entry was a false theorem.

* Fowler, *Some uniform effective results on André–Oort for sums of powers in C^n*, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 180 (2026), 607–641, DOI 10.1017/S0305004125101825. The definition at p607 and monic integral order class polynomial in §2.1, pp612–613, supply integrality. Historical A065 already pins this final PDF. Its prior acceptance concerned its prior uses; the present connection still requires review.
* Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*, second edition, Springer 2009, DOI 10.1007/978-0-387-09494-6. III.1.4(b),(c), p45, supplies algebraic isomorphism and models with prescribed j. VI.4.1 and its corollary, pp171–173, and VI.5.1.1/5.3, pp173/175, supply uniformization and scalar maps. C.11.1/11.2, p426, supplies ideal classes and the Hilbert class field. C.11.4, p427, supplies the inverse-ideal Frobenius formula with its explicit good-reduction hypothesis.
* The official April 2, 2015 Silverman errata, PDF19, corrects the cubic/discriminant expressions in VI.3.6, the differential and divisor signs at p171, and the discriminant condition in VI.5.1 to A^3−27B^2≠0. PDF32 corrects C.11.1's reference to VI.5.1.1. The October 13, 2022 errata, PDF19, adds the image of the origin in VI.3.6(b). These do not alter the uniformization/scalar-map interface. No change to C.11.4 was located in these two pinned lists. This is a bounded check of those lists, not an assertion that no other correction exists.
* Cai–Shu–Tian, *Explicit Gross–Zagier and Waldspurger formulae*, Algebra & Number Theory 8 (2014), 2523–2572, DOI 10.2140/ant.2014.8.2523. Final pp2524,2527,2531 were checked for the ideal-lattice Heegner points, the Artin notation, and Galois/Hecke compatibility. Historical A027 already pins this final PDF. The present direct proof uses Silverman's precise inverse-ideal formula; CST is a comparison record, not a way to omit that convention check or import all height hypotheses.
* Gross–Zagier, *On singular moduli*, J. reine angew. Math. 355 (1985), 191–220, DOI 10.1515/crll.1985.355.191. The final scan, PDF9–10, printed198–199, supplies the general forward inert-to-supersingular sentence. It appears before the specialized small-prime congruence calculation and cites Deuring. Neither Proposition2.7 nor a unique lifting theorem is used. The published Deuring input remains a transitive source of that sentence; this packet does not reprove it.
* Milne, *Class Field Theory*, v4.03, August 6, 2020, Introduction p7 and Chapter I p20, records the arithmetic Frobenius definition already used in A068. This is explicitly an author-course-note source, not a final published book. It supplies only the convention comparison here. C.11.4's printed convention is preserved and the required quadratic class sum is invariant under a single global inverse convention, so no unverified final-source equivalence is required for that sum.

All source PDFs, page renders and extracted full text are local evidence only. Public records contain bibliographic locators, hashes, mathematical paraphrases and the new connecting proof. The newly authored application of these sources is pending independent review; the good-reduction supplement has its own separate accepted scope.
