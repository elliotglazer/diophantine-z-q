## Source-to-claim checks

1. **Hypotheses.** Silverman VII.5.5, printed pp.197 and 199, gives potential good reduction from integral j. The selected Legendre proof applies in every odd residue characteristic, including 3. The proof retains integrality as a separate CM premise. For the quotient and level assertions, the actual integral ideal is prime to qN, so d=Norm(a) is a unit in R and coprime to N. No analogous etaleness conclusion is asserted for q-primary torsion.

2. **Objects.** The finite diagram contains the actual curves, arrows, labelled O-actions and level subgroups. A finite set of action generators and finitely many good-reduction extensions suffice. A finite compositum gives one local field for this diagram. Further base change preserves good reduction. No extension uniform over all torsion or all ideals is claimed.

3. **Domains.** BLR 1.4/2, printed p.19, applies to the smooth proper models over the connected Dedekind scheme Spec(R) and supplies the extending group structure. I checked its statement and proof visually. BLR Definition 1.2/1 and Proposition 1.2/8, printed pp.12 and 15, quantify over smooth source schemes. Both A and A x_R A are such sources. The field, trait, geometric-fiber and strict-henselian comparisons are kept distinct.

4. **Maps.** Unique extension first gives each actual morphism. Uniqueness on the product source then proves addition compatibility; uniqueness for compositions preserves the dual identities and O-equivariance. A section-only extension property would be insufficient, and is not substituted. Silverman III.6.1--6.2 supplies the dual in the reverse direction; uniqueness and Galois invariance give its definition over K.

5. **Identity of the kernel and quotient.** The finite group O/a has order d, hence d lies in a. Every simultaneous a-kernel is therefore contained in A[d]. EGA IV4 18.5.14--15, printed pp.133--134, supplies henselianity and the finite-etale equivalence. After strict henselization, the simultaneous kernel and the extended isogeny kernel are unions of the same components of A[d], because their generic fibers agree. Equality therefore holds as special subgroup schemes. The explicit isomorphism A_bar x ker(f_bar) with A_bar x_(B_bar) A_bar and the finite-etale surjection f_bar identify B_bar with the fppf quotient. No general quotient-existence theorem is silently assumed.

6. **Normalizations and labels.** The degree is the integer d, not its residue modulo q. Silverman III.6.2(d) gives deg[m]=m^2 even if q divides m; thus a nonzero CM endomorphism cannot specialize to the zero morphism. This proves injectivity of the same labelled order action. The standard j-invariant and the actual dual direction are used. Differentiating O-equivariance and cancelling the nonzero tangent map preserves the same residue embedding of O/qO, rather than its conjugate.

7. **Uniformity and degree.** Differentiation of the group law gives d[n]=n on the relative tangent line. Translation carries the calculation to geometric points. EGA IV4 17.11.2(c'), printed p.84, is the appropriate cotangent criterion, without its optional tangent formulation's equal-residue-field assumption. EGA IV4 17.6.2, printed p.71, gives local quasi-finiteness. The graph/projection argument gives properness. EGA III1 4.4.11, printed p.138, then gives finiteness since the target is locally noetherian. EGA IV4 18.2.3 and 18.2.7, printed pp.112--113, give finite local freeness. The author's smooth-openness argument proves B connected, so rank is everywhere the known generic degree d and is positive. This establishes degree and surjectivity on the special fiber.

8. **Exceptional cases and corrections.** I read the 2015 errata's pp.7 and 21 and the relevant 2022 entries. The dual-construction direction, characteristic-zero case distinction, divisor sign/notation, and definition-field clarification are respected. The nearby VII.5.4 and Neron--Ogg--Shafarevich changes are not used as a substitute for the selected Legendre proof. The injectivity argument deliberately permits q dividing deg(alpha); the finite-etale argument deliberately does not. Geometric torsion comparison is not presented as equality of rational torsion over the original K. No absence of all possible source corrections is inferred from a search.

9. **Downstream consumption.** On N-torsion a Bezout coefficient times the dual is a two-sided inverse. It preserves the labelled eigensummands, including prime-power levels. On q-power torsion group schemes, compatible residues of d inverse in Z_q give compatible two-sided inverses; this correctly proves a q-divisible-group isomorphism without etaleness. The tangent character of O/qO is a field embedding because q is inert, and equivariance preserves that character. These are the exact compatibilities needed by the separate class-set construction, not a proof of that construction.

The source statements, their relevant surrounding proofs and the indicated mathematical joins were read. The published BLR proof's Weil extension theorem 4.4/1 remains an identified transitive foundation; this review does not claim to reprove the whole source books. The EGA auxiliary local-quasi-finiteness edge is explicitly present in APPLICATIONS.json and is not hidden inside the EGA III pin.
