# Selected sources of the cohomology bridge

These are authored source projections. The original PDFs remain local-only.

## KUMMER-CURVE

For a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field and n invertible there, H^1(X,mu_n) is naturally Pic^0(X)[n].

## JACOBIAN-POLARIZATION

For the pointed smooth projective curve the theta homomorphism of its Jacobian is an isomorphism. The proof notes invariance of that homomorphism under translating the theta divisor.

## TATE-PAIRING

The prime-to-characteristic torsion of an abelian variety and its dual have compatible nondegenerate pairings into the roots of unity.

## ABELIAN-H1

First integral l-adic cohomology of the geometric abelian variety is canonically dual to its Tate module. For an abelian variety over k the identification is Galois compatible.

## ABELIAN-H1-FACTOR

First integral l-adic cohomology of the geometric abelian variety is canonically dual to its Tate module, compatibly with Galois action.

## CUSP-EXTENSION

For a closed subscheme and its open complement there is a short exact sequence of extension by zero, the given etale sheaf, and the closed pushforward of its restriction.

## TORSOR-H1

Degree-one cohomology with coefficients in an abelian sheaf classifies torsors for that sheaf.

## FINITE-ETALE-TORSOR

A torsor for a finite constant abelian group on the etale site is represented by a finite etale scheme.

## NORM-EXISTS

A finite locally free morphism of positive degree has a canonical norm of that degree compatible with base change.

## PIC-NORM

A norm of degree d for a finite map induces a Picard-group norm whose composite with pullback raises a line bundle to its d-th power.

## KUMMER-TRANSITION

For a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field and n invertible there, H^1(X,mu_n) is naturally Pic^0(X)[n].

## MODULAR-OPEN-TOWER

The open modular curves with the specified finite-adelic levels are defined over Q, with surjective refinement maps. The weight-two local system is constant and the parabolic tower is the direct limit of compact-support images.
