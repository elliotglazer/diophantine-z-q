# Selected level geometry sources

## OPEN

The Q-defined open modular curves Y(K_f) classify the given finite-adelic level structures. Refinement gives a surjection and torsion-free intersection with SL_2(Z) suffices for a fine level.

## COMPACT

A regular curve over a perfect field has a smooth projective compactification with finite complement.

## EXTEND

A rational map from a normal curve to a proper variety extends uniquely to a morphism.

## FINITE

A map from a proper scheme of dimension at most one to a separated target is finite when it is nonconstant on every one-dimensional irreducible component.
