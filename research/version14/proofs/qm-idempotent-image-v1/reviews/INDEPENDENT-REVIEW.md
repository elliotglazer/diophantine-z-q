## Source and elementary proof

The external source is the official digital Stacks Project Lemma 39.8.2, tag 047N, read directly on 2026-11-08 at https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/047N. It asserts smoothness for locally algebraic group schemes over a characteristic-zero field. The author's source statement is a declared projection of that digital source. It is not presented as a printed book excerpt. The constructed image is a finite-type group scheme before this statement is used, so the source's actual hypotheses hold.

For the integral image, properness gives the closed underlying image. The reduced structure allows factorization of the morphism and schematic dominance: a pulled-back-zero section vanishes at every point, hence is zero on the reduced target. The tensor argument is valid over the field k. Finite affine covers of the inverse images test injectivity, and tensoring injections over a field remains injective. This proves schematic dominance for the product without assuming in advance that the product target is reduced. Multiplication and inversion therefore factor through the image. Cartier then supplies smoothness. Geometric connectedness follows from the geometrically connected source and the surjection after base change. Projectivity comes from the closed immersion in the original abelian variety. Thus the result is an abelian subvariety over the same field.

The multiplication argument is also valid. The image of [m] has full dimension because its differential contains the invertible differential of [m]. Its finite-type kernel has zero tangent spaces at every geometric point, hence dimension zero and is finite. An integral homomorphism killed by m has connected smooth image inside this finite kernel, so vanishes. This proves the required torsion-freeness without importing a Tate-module assertion.

After clearing a common denominator, the integral identities follow from this torsion-freeness. Restricting them to the image factors is legitimate by schematic dominance. The addition map p and the tuple q satisfy pq=[m] and qp=[m]. Thus p is surjective with finite kernel and has inverse q/m in rational Hom. These identities also cover a zero idempotent and show independence from the chosen denominator. No integral inverse or specified auxiliary isogeny degree is asserted.

The corner formulas are inverse in both directions. With inclusion j_i and rational projection r_i, the relations r_i j_i=1 and j_i r_i=e_i identify the full rational endomorphism ring of the image with the indicated corner. Full off-diagonal matrix units give inverse rational maps between factors, so these are isogenies over the original field.

For differentiation, a nonzero homomorphism has a positive-dimensional smooth image. In characteristic zero, a transcendence basis and separability prove full generic differential rank onto that image. Translation transports this rank to the identity. The closed immersion of the smooth image in the target has injective tangent map. Consequently the original differential is nonzero. Clearing denominators proves faithful differentiation on rational endomorphisms. This statement is deliberately restricted to characteristic zero.
