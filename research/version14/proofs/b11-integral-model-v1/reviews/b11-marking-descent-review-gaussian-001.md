## Independent source and argument checks

**Torsion and markings.** The field assertions in Stacks 0BFG and 0BFH concern multiplication and its invertibility condition. The proof passes to an abelian scheme using the actual flatness and finite-presentation hypotheses of 039E and 02GM. Properness follows from the graph and proper projection; finite fibres and 02LS then give finiteness. Base change by the zero section gives the finite étale kernel, of rank n^(2g). At g=2 the actual ranks are 3^4 and 5^4. This includes all residue characteristics of Z[1/30], rather than only the generic fibre.

The use of 03RV is appropriate: after a common étale trivialization, addition and the actions of finitely many integral generators of O are finite tables. The bijections satisfying these tables form a finite locally constant sheaf. Representability and finite étaleness do not imply nonemptiness. The latter remains the separate module hypothesis at every geometric fibre, including the five-marking sheaf on every target full-three object. No arbitrary abelian surface is claimed to have the required O-module structure.

**Projective marking space and quotient.** The final correction uses the precise 01W7 Lemma 29.44.14: the terminal base of a composite of projective morphisms must be quasi-compact and quasi-separated in this statement. Here it is Spec Z[1/30], so the hypothesis holds. I read that lemma and its proof in the pinned full HTML, not only the definition excerpt. Together with finite-implies-projective (0B3I), it supplies projectivity of X35. The added direct MARKING dependency records this actual input to QUOTIENT.

The geometric stabilizer input is sufficient for this finite constant group, by the fixed-subscheme argument now written in section 4. For each nonidentity element the closed equalizer has no geometric point and hence is empty. The identity equalizer is X35. Locally constant group sections therefore have trivial stabilizer on every test scheme, including nonreduced ones. This is the scheme-theoretic free-action hypothesis of 07S7. No inversion of the group order is used. That source gives a scheme quotient and an fppf torsor, not projectivity by itself. The finite étale conclusion follows after torsor trivialization and descent.

For the separatedness argument the graphs are not just closed: their disjointness follows from freeness, so their finite disjoint union is the pulled-back closed diagonal. Smoothness, the closed diagonal, universal closedness and quasi-compactness give the asserted smooth proper quotient.
