# Buzzard marking sheaf: selected published assertion

Source: Kevin Buzzard, Integral models of certain Shimura curves, Duke Mathematical Journal 87 (1997), 591–612, DOI 10.1215/S0012-7094-97-08719-6.
Final source SHA256 ed34a072898d0e6ef6526c877159fbe627ae695265d4f5b2f2a1d6e35c9bac65; Definition 1.1 and following paragraph on printed p593. Source PDF remains local-only.

For M prime to the discriminant, with M inverted on the base, the full-M O-linear marking sheaf is an etale torsor under the level-module unit group; an H-orbit marking is represented by the associated finite etale quotient. Pulling the full torsor back along an H-orbit marking gives its finite etale surjective H-torsor of refinements.
Here M=5, d=6 and H defines V1(5). A marking is of a left O-module. Its relabeling uses right multiplication: g dot m = m composed with r_g. All determinant components are retained. This assertion is not applied at M=3, since 3 divides d.
