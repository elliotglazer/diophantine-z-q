# LP02 geometric splitting successor

Author /root/sol_protocol_final_review. Supersedes R2 for the LP02 splitting justification. R2 and its review record remain preserved.

The review asked for a fixed finite-step lift and proof that the two quotient maps separate points after passing to lattice cosets. The R3 proof constructs a free Lie algebra on positive-weight generators and kills words outside the allowed downset. It uses a homogeneous BCH lattice, with scaling chosen both for subgroup closure and for image in the original lattice. The projected lattices are exact coordinate projections. Thus equality in both quotient spaces forces every logarithmic coordinate into the original lattice. Merely knowing that the ideals intersect trivially would not establish this.

The needed uniform test decomposition follows from this fixed compact geometry, Stone–Weierstrass and a finite net. The J0 quotient's degree-zero and degree-one groups are equal because its surviving generators have positive second weight. Hence the top group is central for the Fourier decomposition. The proof checks that property before invoking E.5.
