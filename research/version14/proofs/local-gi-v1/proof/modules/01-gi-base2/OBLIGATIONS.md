# GI base application comparisons

Status: author proposal under protocol1.4; independent review pending.

## BASE-O01: Source hypotheses

Required: The published forward bracket theorem must match the cyclic input.
Constructed: Prime P, complex one-bounded u, fixed eta and the exact (10.7)/(10.9) output.
Connecting derivation and scope: Section 1 states the needed published output; Section 2 replaces its affected H={0} specialization, leaving other published inputs explicit.

## BASE-O02: Objects

Required: Output must use fixed connected simply connected rational filtered models.
Constructed: Products of circle, two-torus and connected Heisenberg models with explicit degree-two filtrations.
Connecting derivation and scope: Section 4 constructs the groups, quotients, tests and orbits directly. It does not identify the disconnected skew group with a connected group.

## BASE-O03: Domains

Required: Algebraic identities and correlation must hold on all required integers and Bohr sets.
Constructed: An all-integer bracket identity and exact P-periodic product; every finite-box cube is covered in the H={0} proof.
Connecting derivation and scope: The product periodicity is proved before cyclic translation, so no signed-representative gap remains.

## BASE-O04: Maps

Required: Right quotient reduction and polynomial derivatives must identify the actual test.
Constructed: Heisenberg coordinate w-u[v], explicit first and second left differences, and the translated product orbit.
Connecting derivation and scope: The calculation supplies exactly chi(alpha n)e(-beta n[alpha n]); the other factors give the exact bracket expansion.

## BASE-O05: Uniformity

Required: The family and Lipschitz/correlation bounds must be independent of N, frequencies and coefficients.
Constructed: d is bounded in eta; product types are fixed; integer coefficient q enters only the orbits; cutoff parameters depend only on eta.
Connecting derivation and scope: Each test has bound C(1+Lip chi); finite products and finitely many d values give fixed final bounds.

## BASE-O06: Constants

Required: Retain all cutoff, norm and correlation factors.
Constructed: Shell error Cd epsilon |B|, Lipschitz factor of order 1/(epsilon rho), and indicator norm at least N/P.
Connecting derivation and scope: Choose epsilon after the positive b_star and eta after delta,C. No universal numerical bound 50 or 2pi is imported.

## BASE-O07: Exceptions

Required: Small N, empty S, small box sides and diagonal pairs must be covered.
Constructed: Bertrand applies to CN>1; empty S uses a constant test; sides 1 and 2 are handled; quadratic pairs j=k are allowed.
Connecting derivation and scope: Each case is explicit in Sections 2-5 and changes no fixed-family bound.

## BASE-O08: Consumed conclusion

Required: The output must be the polynomial GI(2) base used by the induction.
Constructed: A connected degree-at-most-two model, one-bounded test and fixed positive interval correlation.
Connecting derivation and scope: Section 5 derives the normalized interval conclusion. Same-step linearization is not required for this polynomial base interface.
