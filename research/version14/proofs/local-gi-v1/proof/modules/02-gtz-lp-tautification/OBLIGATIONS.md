# Comparisons for tautification and symbol shift

Author comparisons under protocol v1.4. All await independent review. Each section addresses GTZ-LP-TAUTIFICATION and GTZ-LP-SYMBOL-SHIFT to the extent stated. The exact proof and source-use spans are bound in APPLICATIONS.json; these links supply the derivations without duplicating the proofs.

## Source hypotheses

Required: a finite rational filtered nilmanifold for one of the three specified domain filtrations. The shift application also needs a positive-degree internal nilcharacter and an allowed positive-index domain direction. Constructed: the original G_i, Gamma and polynomial, with standard internal vertical eta, followed by the exact taut representation of Theorem 1. Theorem 1 checks inherited filtration axioms. Theorem 3 uses i=(1,0) for degree-rank, preserving its additional equalities; it does not use E.8(iv) to prove itself. Evidence: TAUT-U01, U03, U04 and proof Theorems 1 and 3.

## Constructed objects

Required: a fixed taut rational nilmanifold representing the original sequence and a lower-degree nilmanifold representing its shifted tensor. Constructed: H=G1, the group generated by G_ej, or G_(1,0), with lattice H intersect Gamma; then L_j generated by U(G_(j+i)) and D(G_j), Delta=L0 intersect Gamma^2, and the quotient by L_d. The linked rational-subgroup proof supplies connectedness, simple connectedness and lattice cocompactness. Theorem 3 proves the pair commutators before taking the quotient. Evidence: TAUT-U02 and U05, Theorems 1 and 3.

## Domain and index coverage

Required: polynomial membership for every integer input and all target indices, including zero. Constructed: coordinate-step telescoping puts h(n) in H for every n, and each positive G_i lies in H. Theorem 1 checks zeroth-order membership separately and retains every positive derivative group. Theorem 3 covers both shifted and diagonal factors for every index, checks index-zero commutators, and checks the degree-rank equalities. There is no bounded-box restriction. Evidence: TAUT-U01, U03, U05 and both proofs.

## Transition and comparison maps

Required: normalization, restriction and quotient must be compatible, with no missing scalar or lattice conjugation. Constructed: g0=q gamma, h=q^-1 g gamma^-1, F_q(x)=F(qx), the compact subnilmanifold inclusion, U and D, and L0 to L0/L_d. The identities F_q(h(n)Gamma)=F(g(n)Gamma) and U(partial_u g)D(g)=(g(n+u),g(n)) prove compatibility. The quotient lemma proves that the image lattice is discrete and cocompact. Evidence: TAUT-U02, U03, U05 and both proofs.

## Identity of the consumed object

Required: the output must equal the original sequence, or chi(n+u) tensor conjugate chi(n), rather than a proportional or approximate object. Constructed: restricted F_q on h, and the two-factor test T on the exact pair orbit P_u. The displayed identities identify their values at each map. The vertical characters in T cancel on D(G_d), so descent and final tautification preserve those same values. Evidence: TAUT-U02 and U05; normalization in Theorem 1 and quotient paragraph in Theorem 3.

## Coefficient and normalization conventions

Required: the standard real vertical homomorphism, tensor conjugation and all-limit domains remain valid, and degree bounds do not increase. Constructed: eta remains unchanged by q; T=F tensor conjugate F; positive groups remain fixed; the quotient kills the maximal index d. Integer-valued continuity on connected G0 and lattice spanning prove eta_q=eta. Natural ultrapower relations are explicit; exact limit equivariance is not inferred from standard-only tests. Actual support at zero can shrink, so the theorem claims no larger support. Evidence: TAUT-U01, U02, U04, U05 and the convention paragraph plus both proofs.

## Local conditions

Required: rationality, compactness and compatibility of lattice intersections hold at every filtration group and quotient; parameter choices preserve bounds. Constructed: exact intersections and a central rational quotient, together with one fixed bounded representative section. Each positive group has its original lattice. The linked quotient lemma supplies the image lattice. Smooth compact inclusions and fixed quotient metrics give uniform Lipschitz bounds. These are deterministic Lie-group constructions; no probabilistic stopping or change of law occurs. Evidence: TAUT-U02 and U05 and the geometric paragraphs in both proofs.

## Exceptional cases

Required: trivial positive groups, zero shift, non-taut original G0 and higher degree-rank indices with trivial domain group must be covered. Constructed: a point nilmanifold when H is trivial; the same pair quotient for u=0; i=(1,0) in the degree-rank case even when the originally specified positive domain group is trivial. Theorem 1 allows support at zero to shrink. Theorem 3 does not claim a vector tensor at zero shift is constant. Top centrality is proved after tautification, rather than assumed for the original G0. Evidence: TAUT-U01, U02, U03, U05, Theorem 1, Corollary 2 and Theorem 3.

Theorem 1 supplies exact tautification for the three stated domains. E.4/E.5 splitting, vector-component selection and the two Cauchy–Schwarz steps remain separate dependencies; neither application is declared accepted by this unit. Evidence: TAUT-U04 and U05 and the final paragraph of the symbol-shift section.
