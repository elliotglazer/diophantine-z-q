# Section 12 geometric join

This is an authored project proof of the semidirect group, rational lattice,
polynomial orbit and global unit-vector chart construction needed at the end
of GTZ Section 12. It depends on the exact LP04 Hall/petal interface and on
published Corollary B.4. It retains the additional hypotheses of the LP04
comparison. Independent mathematical review is pending.

Read `proof.tex` and `APPLICATION.json` for the exact conclusion and limits.
`SOURCE-USES.json` pins each substantive import and its available-to-needed
connection. `RELATED-USE-AUDIT.json` records the bounded affected occurrences.
`SOURCES.json` also pins the working assembly snapshot as a consumer, not an
accepted supplier. The manifest binds exact bytes; it does not approve them.

The character must be extended and the new test chosen before the LP04
comparison. The final output is exactly the original test with zero padding
on a dense subfamily. At rank greater than one, the comparison remains a
degree-rank drop. The rank iteration, analytic inputs and complete Theorem 7.1
assembly are separate obligations.

No live manuscript was changed. The external PDF and images in `local-only`
are local reference material and are excluded from the distributable manifest.
Build success is a formatting check, not proof acceptance.
