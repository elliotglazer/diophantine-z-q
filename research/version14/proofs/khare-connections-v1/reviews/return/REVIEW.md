## Type separation

The characteristic-zero input is precisely a rank-two potentially crystalline representation with weights (0,1) and tame type 1 plus omega_tilde^j, for even 2<=j<=P-3. These characters factor through niveau one. Their exponents as powers of omega_tilde_2 are divisible by P+1. In Savitt 6.12 the exponents are m and Pm with P+1 not dividing m. ## The actual lattice and local alternatives

Savitt 2.10-2.13 use the covariant convention D_st,2(V)=D_st(V(-1)), with lambda_a sending arithmetic Frobenius to a. Example 2.13 gives Frobenius eigenvalue P^(1-i)*a^(-1) for epsilon^i omega_tilde^j lambda_a. This verifies the normalization directly.

For an indecomposable representation, 2.17/2.20 give the stated diagonal Frobenius and descent action and filtration line. At an endpoint where x1 is a unit, E e1 has induced filtration concentrated in degree zero and Newton degree zero, so is weakly admissible. Its actual subrepresentation is epsilon*lambda_(x1^-1); the quotient is omega_tilde^j*lambda_(x1/w). With x2 a unit the corresponding subrepresentation is epsilon*omega_tilde^j*lambda_(x2^-1), with quotient lambda_(x2/w). These formulas also agree with the ordered residual matrices in 6.11(1)-(2). They follow from a subobject of the characteristic-zero representation, not an exchange of residual semisimplification factors.

Intersecting that line with the actual lattice gives a saturated free rank-one submodule and free quotient over the coefficient DVR. Reduction therefore preserves the asserted orientation. A decomposable representation is treated by its rank-one weight-zero and weight-one summands. No classification of all exceptional endpoint lattices is needed.

If both slopes are in (0,1), 6.9 and 6.11(3) give a lattice with inertia omega_2^(1+j) plus omega_2^(P(1+j)). In this range 3<=1+j<=P-2, so the characters are genuinely niveau two and exchanged by Frobenius. The local reduction is absolutely irreducible. Independence of residual semisimplification from the lattice, as also explained by Remark 6.17, transfers this to the supplied lattice. This does not impose a new trivial-endomorphism hypothesis on the original reduction. The exceptional cases excluded by 6.8 have j=1 or j=P-2 and lie outside the stated even range.
