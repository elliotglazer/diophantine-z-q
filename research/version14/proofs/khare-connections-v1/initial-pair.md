# The initial ordinary weight-two lift and its first auxiliary member

Author: /root/astra_quartic_opt05_anchor. This is an application of exact published construction statements, with new hypothesis and consumer connections. It requires independent review. It does not assert a new independent proof of every theorem cited by those statements.

## 1. The consumed construction

Let P>=7 be prime. Let r:G_Q->GL_2(k) be continuous, odd and absolutely irreducible with nonsolvable image, unramified at every finite prime other than P. Suppose its actual Serre weight k_r is even and 8<=k_r<=P+1. Suppose it is ordinary at P, with inertia upper triangular with diagonal (omega_P^(k_r-1),1). The ordinary reduction and this weight range are upstream inputs; this packet does not manufacture them by twisting an arbitrary representation.

There exists a number field E and an E-rational odd irreducible almost strictly compatible system A, lifting this particular r at the fixed P-adic embedding, such that A_P is an ordinary weight-two representation, unramified outside P. It has determinant omega_P^(k_r-2)*epsilon_P. Its inertial Weil-Deligne parameter is (omega_P^(k_r-2) plus1,0) if k_r<P+1, and (id,N) with N nonzero if k_r=P+1. Here omega denotes the finite Teichmuller lift when used in a characteristic-zero formula; epsilon is the cyclotomic character. For every odd l different from P, A_l is absolutely irreducible, odd, finitely ramified and crystalline at l of weights(0,1), unramified outside P and l, and det A_l=omega_{P,l}^(k_r-2)*epsilon_l. The finite factor is unramified at l.

This supplies the actual initial pair required by the five small cases and the general FIRST construction. The pair is chosen together. It does not assert that every previously prescribed ordinary lift admits such a system, nor does it construct the second family with changed P-type.

## 2. The residual and determinant checks

First r restricted to G_Q(mu_P) is absolutely irreducible. Indeed restriction of a finite-image irreducible representation to a normal subgroup is semisimple. If the restriction were a sum of two different characters, its two eigenspaces would be permuted by the full image. A subgroup of index at most two would be diagonal, making the image solvable. If the restriction were scalar, the remaining quotient is cyclic, since Gal(Q(mu_P)/Q) is cyclic. The full image would then be generated by scalar matrices and one matrix, also solvable. Both alternatives contradict the hypothesis. This argument does not assume that the projective image is a particular PSL_2 group.

The finite character det(r)*omega_P^(1-k_r) is unramified at P by the given ordinary inertia, and is unramified away from P. A finite character of G_Q unramified at every finite prime is trivial: its finite abelian extension would have discriminant of absolute value one, which is impossible for a nontrivial number field. Hence det(r)=omega_P^(k_r-1).

Choose the global arithmetic character psi=omega_P^(k_r-2). It is finite, even and unramified away from P; psi*epsilon_P lifts det(r). This is a permitted fixed determinant in KWII Section10.1. The case k_r=P+1 gives psi=1. All primes P in the actual small list7,11,19,29,31 and every general prime P>31 are in this domain. No construction in residual characteristic2 or3 is asserted here.

## 3. Apply the published deformation construction with this local type

Set S={P,infinity}. At infinity choose odd deformations. At P choose the weight-two local deformation problem of KWII Section3.2.2(i), not its low-weight crystalline problem. For k_r<P+1 the ordinary residual case is Proposition3.6, printed524. Its proof writes the lifts with diagonal characters chi_1*eta_1 and eta_2, where chi_1=epsilon_P*omega_P^(k_r-2), the eta_i are unramified, and their product gives the fixed determinant. Thus the quotient is genuinely unramified, with the specified residual quotient. The residual inertia is nontrivial in this domain, so its stated smooth ordinary case applies. For k_r=P+1 use the semistable weight-two problem in Section3.2.6, printed529. It fixes the unramified character gamma and yields the shape (gamma*epsilon_P,star;0,gamma). The required nonzero monodromy is the endpoint case in Section3.2.2 and KWI5.1(2). We do not replace it by a crystalline endpoint lift.

The local rings meet Theorem3.1, printed519: over Q_P the field is unramified, the selected rings have the required flatness and relative dimensions, and the infinite-place deformation is odd. The global image ring Rbar^psi_S is exactly the ring of KWII Section10.1 for these local conditions. Theorem10.1, printed581-582, makes it finite over Z_P. Corollary4.7, printed542, then produces a characteristic-zero point in a finite coefficient extension. Its statement also gives the lift to the framed/local-condition ring; this is not an unsupported assumption that every point of an image ring extends to an arbitrary overring. Section10.3.1, printed583, explicitly identifies these points with lifts of the required type.

This gives the actual ordinary weight-two A_P, not just a local lift or a deformation of a different Hodge type. The lifting ring contains no auxiliary ramification outside S. The argument does not assume that r is modular over Q. The modular forms used in the published finiteness proof occur only after the potential-modularity base change specified there.

## 4. Choose its compatible system and read the correct definition

KWII Section10.3.2, printed583-584, propagates the chosen point to the system in KWI Theorem5.1(2), printed492. KWI's definition on491 specifies the original P-adic embedding in the meaning of lifting r, and calls the system irreducible when every member is irreducible. The representations there have coefficients in algebraic closures, so this is absolute irreducibility. Enlarge E if necessary to contain the finite values of psi and the selected embeddings.

The system is almost strictly compatible, not generically declared strictly compatible. The exact additional clause on491 says that if l is not2 and the parameter at l is unramified, the l-adic member is crystalline with the stated weights, without requiring irreducibility of its reduction. The first clause instead gives geometricity and full parameter comparison at the coefficient prime when the reduction is irreducible. These clauses must not be interchanged.

Since A_P is unramified at every l different from P, the common WD parameter at l is unramified. For every odd auxiliary l different from P, the second clause therefore proves precisely the crystalline weight-two assertion required by FIRST, even if its reduction is reducible or solvable. For a finite v different from P and l, compatibility away from the coefficient prime makes A_l unramified at v. Oddness and absolute irreducibility are included in the theorem.

Finally det A_l=psi_l*epsilon_l follows from the equality of good Frobenius determinants with A_P and continuity/density, after placing the finite character values in E. The character psi_l is unramified at l because its conductor divides P. Thus every hypothesis of the new conditional FIRST lifting connection in caller audit13 is supplied by this construction except residual modularity, which remains a separate branch premise. That connection has its own independent decision; it is not used to construct A or prove the published existence theorem.
