# Published sources for the direct prime-five proof

The immediate theorem is the published statement reported by Frank Calegari, *Reciprocity in the Langlands program since Fermat’s Last Theorem*, Proceedings ICM2022, vol.2 (EMS2023), 610–651, definitions611–612 and statement624, DOI10.4171/ICM2022/101. The theorem is due to Pan. This is not a final-Pan-body comparison.

- Peter Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*, Forum Math. Pi1(2013), e1: Corollary1.8, pp5–6; Theorems9.1 and9.3, pp74–75. DOI10.1017/fmp.2013.1. The final2016 corrigendum, Forum Math. Pi4,e6, DOI10.1017/fmp.2016.4, changes the indicated covers/period-sheaf definitions and retains the comparison results.
- Jean-Marc Fontaine, *Représentations p-adiques semi-stables*, Astérisque223(1994),113–184: Theorem3.8,p142, for exact tensor, dual and subobject closure with filtrations.
- J.S. Milne, *Introduction to Shimura varieties*, Clay Math. Proceedings4(2005),265–378: complex uniformization and Theorem6.8/Corollary6.9,p319. The finite-torsion/conjugation connection is proved explicitly in PROOF.md.
- J.S. Milne, *Abelian varieties*, Arithmetic Geometry, Springer1986,103–150: Theorem15.1(a),p129, identifies étale H1 with the dual Tate module. The usual polarization pairing supplies the Tate determinant.
- Gerd Faltings, *Complements to Mordell*, Rational Points, second edition1986: Theorem1(a)–(b),p204, supplies semisimplicity and the full endomorphism commutant.
- L.C. Washington, *Introduction to Cyclotomic Fields*, second edition1997: Theorem14.1,p321, supplies the finite Dirichlet-character realization.
- Goro Shimura, *On the factors of the jacobian variety of a modular function field*, JMSJ25(1973),523–544: Section4,equation4.1,p531, supplies the weight-two translate/Gauss-sum formula. The nebentype and good-prime Hecke identities are the explicit connecting calculation in PROOF.md, not a stronger literal quotation of that page.
- Wen-Ch’ing Li, *Newforms and functional equations*, Math. Ann.212(1975),285–315: p294 and Theorem5,p298, supply primitive extraction at fixed weight and character.
- The classical modular Galois realization, its de Rham weights in Tate normalization, and its finite weight-two determinant character are retained published modular-form inputs. The exact weight and determinant argument is in PROOF.md; it does not assume a conductor bound on the twisting character.
- Chebotarev and characteristic-zero semisimplicity identify the representations from their good-prime traces. No reduction-mod-p trace lemma is substituted.

The bounded existing BG05 finite-twist decision and the older weight/determinant helper decision are reused only for their stated interfaces. Their larger GL2/Serre construction is not a premise of this direct proof. Neither this source list nor the published theorem citation claims a recursive proof audit of every ancestor.
