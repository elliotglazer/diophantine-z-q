# Direct prime-five modularity after rational scalar descent

## 1. Exact object and immediate input

Preserve the statement of Lemma qm-modularity-descent: A is an abelian surface over a finite Galois extension of Q with full geometric endomorphism algebra a quaternion division algebra B/Q. The B-compatible quasi-isogenies mu_sigma form a finite locally constant choice, mu_1=1, and their scalar cocycle is c(sigma,tau) in Q*. A locally constant cochain t:G_Q -> C* in a number field satisfies t_sigma t_tau c(sigma,tau)=t_(sigma tau), with trivial Galois action on added scalars. Enlarge C to split B and fix C -> Qbar_5. The genuine representation rho_5=t_sigma mu_sigma sigma decomposes as r5 direct-sum r5 through the matrix units of B. The asserted output is that this same r5 is the Tate realization of a primitive weight-two cuspidal newform after finite coefficient extension. No controlled field is replaced.

The immediate published modularity statement is Calegari, Reciprocity in the Langlands program since Fermat's Last Theorem, Proceedings ICM2022 vol.2 (EMS2023), pp611-612 and624, DOI10.4171/ICM2022/101. At p>=5 a continuous irreducible geometric two-dimensional representation of G_Q that is odd and has distinct Hodge-Tate weights is modular up to twist. Geometric includes finite ramification and de Rham/potentially semistable local behavior. The p624 statement has no residual irreducibility condition. This is a final published report of Pan's theorem, not a claim to have compared Pan's author-v2 body to the final JAMS body. SOURCE-STATUS.md separates proof ancestry from the immediate published theorem input.

## 2. Finite restriction, irreducibility, ramification

Since mu_1=1, c(1,1)=1; the cochain identity and nonzero t_1 give t_1=1. Local constancy therefore supplies an open identity neighborhood on which mu and t are both 1. Choose a finite Galois extension F/Q whose Galois subgroup lies in that neighborhood and over which A and all its geometric endomorphisms are defined. The restriction of rho_5 to G_F is the actual V_5(A), after extension of coefficients. This F is qualitative, not the controlled L' of either application.

Choose a finite extension E/Q_5 containing the fixed image of C and the finite matrices needed. The Tate restriction to the open subgroup G_F is continuous; its finitely many translates show continuity on G_Q. Faltings semisimplicity and the full endomorphism theorem give End_(G_F)(V_5(A) tensor E)=B tensor E=M_2(E). Since V_5(A) tensor E is two copies of r5|G_F, its matrix commutant is M_2(End_(G_F)(r5)). Thus End_(G_F)(r5)=E. Intertwiner kernels commute with scalar extension (a finite basis of their defining linear equations suffices), and semisimplicity persists in characteristic zero. A reducible semisimple representation over Ebar would have a nontrivial commuting projection. Hence r5|G_F, and therefore r5, is absolutely irreducible. Good reduction of A away from finitely many primes gives unramified Tate action there; adding the finitely many primes ramified in F/Q proves finite ramification for r5. These are the same full-End and ramification inputs used in the two current consumers.

## 4. Oddness for this rational-isogeny descent

Fix an embedding Qbar -> C and the corresponding complex conjugation c. Realize the same descent operator t_c mu_c c on H_1(A(C),Q) tensor C. Complex conjugation interchanges the two Hodge summands; the holomorphic quasi-isogeny mu_c preserves their types, as does the added scalar. Consequently this operator has trace zero. Complex uniformization identifies finite torsion with the homology lattice modulo its integer multiple, functorially for homomorphisms and for complex conjugation. Clearing the denominators of the quasi-isogeny and passing to the 5-power inverse limit therefore identifies its trace with the trace on V_5(A), under the fixed coefficient embedding. Since rho_5 is r5 direct-sum r5, tr(r5(c))=0. The cocycle equation gives r5(c)^2=1; characteristic zero then gives eigenvalues1,-1 and det(r5(c))=-1. This proves oddness for the actual descended representation rather than for a substituted object. Milne2005 Theorem6.8/Corollary6.9 and the preceding complex-uniformization paragraph, printed319, supply the lattice and Hodge description. Explicitly, multiplication by 5^n identifies (5^-n Lambda)/Lambda with Lambda/5^n Lambda. The exponential map commutes with holomorphic group maps and with conjugation to the conjugate variety; this proves the stated functorial torsion comparison. Milne1986 Theorem15.1(a), printed129, supplies the etale dual identification.

## 5. Published modularity, weight two, finite twist

The preceding paragraphs verify every hypothesis of the final Calegari statement at p=5. Thus, after finite coefficient extension, r5 is U_f tensor delta for the normalized primitive classical cuspidal eigenform f, written in Tate normalization; the primitive choice is made before twisting, so a_1(f)=1. Passing between the usual cohomological and Tate normalizations is permitted inside the theorem's up-to-character conclusion; in dimension two the dual differs by the determinant character. Write k for the positive classical weight of f, so U_f has weights0,1-k.

The character delta is the Galois-stable one-dimensional subrepresentation of Hom(U_f,r5) corresponding to the displayed isomorphism. Since both representations are de Rham, Fontaine's tensor/dual/subobject closure makes delta de Rham. If its weight is m, the equality of weight multisets is {m,m+1-k}={0,-1}. Therefore k=2 and m=0.

It remains essential to prove that the twist is finite. Put alpha=det(r5)/epsilon_5. The Weil pairing over F gives det(V_5(A))=epsilon_5^2, hence alpha^2=1 on G_F. A continuous character finite on an open finite-index subgroup has finite total image, so alpha is finite. The character det(U_f)/epsilon_5 is the finite nebentype in this Tate convention. Taking determinants in r5=U_f tensor delta shows delta^2 is finite; a characteristic-zero character whose square has finite image itself has finite image. Thus delta is finite. This does not assume that the original cochain t is a character or invoke a local-algebraicity theorem.
