Apply the ordinary global-test approximation used in GTZ Lemma6.4, as already spelled out in the S13 selection proof: approximate within half the correlation divided by max(1,||u|| infinity), retain the finite coefficient sum, and select one ordinary globally equivariant unit-test component with positive standard correlation. This constructs pointwise witnesses without asserting a common model or bound yet.

Use exactly the ordinary increasing parameter sets T_M of that proof for degree-at-most d models: first M model/character/dimension tags, ordinary global unit tests of Lipschitz bound at most M, unrestricted compatible Taylor coefficients, coordinate and conjugation tags, and integer threshold denominator at most M. It is internal because H,J, the scalar weights, evaluation and the finite sums are internal; the norm-one/global-equivariance conditions are part of the ordinary test parameter set. No inverse theorem, external positive-standard predicate, or bound on orbit coefficients is used in this step.
