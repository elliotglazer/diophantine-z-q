# GTZ-POLYNOMIAL-GROUP — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green, Terence Tao and Tamar Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm, Annals of Mathematics 176 (2012), 1231–1372. Final publisher PDF SHA256 cd320f5c1217df9bb41395e847ae2a924e70521021ca1b01c7abc20550123be1. Locator: Corollary B.4, Proposition B.6 and Corollary B.7, pp.1334–1336.

**statement**: Polynomial maps between the stated I-filtered groups form a group under pointwise operations. The groups of maps into shifted target filtrations form a filtration. Differences of products, and differences in products of directions, are additive modulo the specified deeper commutator-error group.

**objects**: I-filtered groups H,G; P_i=poly(H to G^{+i}); difference operators and the quotient by P_{i+j+k} or P_{i+k+l}.

**hypotheses**: The index system and all source, target and shifted filtrations satisfy the hypotheses of Appendix B; direction h_k lies in H_k. Commutation of the two first differences in B.7 additionally uses an abelian domain.

**quantifiers**: All admissible filtration indices and all polynomial maps and directions in the stated groups.

**parameter range**: The filtered-group setting of Appendix B, including the finite degree, degree-rank and multidegree specializations only after their axioms are checked.

**uniformity**: Exact group identities, independent of polynomial coefficients or any interval length.

**constants**: No analytic estimate or loss is asserted.

**normalizations**: Use the published difference convention consistently; products retain their order. P_i brackets with P_j into P_{i+j}.

**exceptional cases**: No arbitrary Taylor factors are asserted to commute. The printed malformed codomain in B.5 is not imported.

**limit order**: Verify the actual filtrations first, apply product/shifted closure, then compute the indicated quotient errors.

**Application boundary**: Horizontal Taylor homomorphisms, translation invariance modulo rank errors, and derivative convention changes are local deductions in the consuming proofs. This source does not supply those conclusions without the relevant quotient calculation.
