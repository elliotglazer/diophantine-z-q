# CTS selected published assertion

New author projection for ordinary inventory review. This is not the original publication or an independent acceptance.

title: Arithmetic on some singular cubic hypersurfaces
authors: J.-L. Colliot-Thelene and P. Salberger
venue: Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (3) 58 (1989), 519-549
authoritative_version: Published facsimile; SHA256 cc6559e41a0ca0e2cd6b68e37d10b6e086d74c7837258f2eee5c8e699eb7a30b
locator: Section 1, standing hypotheses p520 and Propositions 1.2-1.3 p521
corrections: No whole-paper or recursive ancestor certification. The proof invokes CTSSD1987 Remark2.3.1; its named earlier inputs, including then-preprint Coray-Tsfasman, remain recorded.

statement: A geometrically integral nonconical cubic hypersurface with a rational point is unirational over its characteristic-zero field.
objects: X in projective n-space over k and a dominant rational parametrization.
hypotheses: Characteristic zero; n>=3; geometric integrality; noncone; X(k) nonempty.
quantifiers: For every such k and X.
parameter_range: Dimension at least two; applied only over Q.
uniformity: The assertion applies to each object satisfying the stated hypotheses; no uniform effective bound is used.
constants: No quantitative constant is supplied or changed.
normalizations: Cone means a projective vertex after scalar extension; rational points need not be smooth.
exceptional_cases: Singular rational points and codimension-one singular loci are included. Plane cubics and cones are excluded.
limit_order: No limiting operation is used.
