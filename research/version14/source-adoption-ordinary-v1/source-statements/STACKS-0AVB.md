# Reflexive modules and codimension-one intersections

Source: The Stacks Project, Lemma15.24.18, Tag0AVB, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AVB. HTML retrieved2026-11-09T08:44:25.999192Z; SHA2566f9faf4957a95a56f690bf80631fdabe7071966f7dc30ea3bf1c71d427d74327.

For a Noetherian normal domain R with fraction field K, a finite reflexive R-module M equals the intersection of its localizations M_p over height-one primes, inside M tensor_R K.

This is a source declaration. The application to sections of an invertible sheaf on regular etale charts, and their descent to the actual maximal stack, is recorded separately.
