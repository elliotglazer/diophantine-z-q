# GTZ-UNIT-VERTICAL — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Ben Green, Terence Tao and Tamar Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm, Annals of Mathematics 176 (2012), 1231–1372. Final publisher PDF SHA256 cd320f5c1217df9bb41395e847ae2a924e70521021ca1b01c7abc20550123be1. Locator: Section 6, formula (6.3), p.1254; proof of Lemma 6.4, p.1256; proof of E.5, p.1353.

**statement**: For a fixed compact nilmanifold with rational central top torus and an integral top character, finitely many local trivializations and a smooth partition with sum of squared magnitudes one produce a globally defined smooth unit-vector test with that vertical character.

**objects**: A central torus bundle G/Gamma over G/(Gtop Gamma), an integral character of its top torus, and a finite-dimensional complex vector-valued test.

**hypotheses**: Centrality and rationality of the top subgroup; integrality of the character on its lattice; compact base admitting finite smooth local sections.

**quantifiers**: Every fixed integral top character on the stated fixed bundle.

**parameter range**: Finite-dimensional compact nilmanifold and fixed character; the vector dimension may depend on those data.

**uniformity**: Global equivariance on all ordinary points and a finite Lipschitz bound on the fixed model.

**constants**: No bound uniform over unbounded characters or changing models is asserted.

**normalizations**: The vector has pointwise Euclidean norm one; the squared partition coefficients sum to one.

**exceptional cases**: A global scalar function of modulus one need not exist. Zero coordinates are permitted; no coordinate is divided out.

**limit order**: Choose the actual character and finite bundle atlas, construct the ordinary global unit test, then extend it when forming represented limit families.

**Application boundary**: The strong all-limit identity comes from extension of these ordinary globally equivariant tests, not from silently strengthening the literal quantifiers of Definition 6.19. Extension of a rank-top character to the full ordinary top torus is a separate local Hall-lattice step.
