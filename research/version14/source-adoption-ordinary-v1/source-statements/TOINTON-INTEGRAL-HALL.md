# TOINTON-INTEGRAL-HALL — bounded source statement

This is a project paraphrase, not the publication.

Source: Matthew C. H. Tointon, Introduction to Approximate Groups, Cambridge University Press, LMS Student Texts 94 (2020). Author-hosted published book PDF SHA256 16e53474390645cf0256c92fb821c08c4cadffe3b31e5c685f5bb023b1208d59. Locator: Definition 5.4.3, Theorem 5.4.5 and the final free-nilpotent paragraph of Section 5.4, printed pp.96–98.

**statement**: In a free nilpotent group the ordered basic commutators give unique integer collection coordinates; basic commutators of each bracket length give a free abelian basis of the corresponding lower-central quotient.

**objects**: A free nilpotent group on finitely many generators, ordered basic commutators and its lower-central series.

**hypotheses**: The free group and nilpotency-class hypotheses of Section 5.4; use the stated basic-commutator order.

**quantifiers**: Every element of the free nilpotent group and every lower-central layer through its fixed class.

**parameter range**: Finite rank and finite nilpotency class. The source weight here is bracket length.

**uniformity**: Integral unique coordinates for the whole free nilpotent group at fixed rank and class.

**constants**: No analytic constants.

**normalizations**: Integer powers of group commutators; the associated graded classes are a free abelian basis.

**exceptional cases**: This is not a saturation theorem for an arbitrary rational quotient or arbitrary weighted ideal.

**limit order**: Collect in the free group first; justify each special quotient and lattice intersection separately.

**Application boundary**: The project proof uses triangular BCH coordinates and upward-closed individual-letter multidegree monomial ideals to establish its exact quotient lattice. That local saturation argument is essential. The previously checked author corrections list had no correction to Section 5.4; this is not a claim about all future errata. The book PDF remains local and is not redistributed.
