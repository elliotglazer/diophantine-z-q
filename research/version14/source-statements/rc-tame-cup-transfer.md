# Tame cup products and transfer

These paragraphs paraphrase retained published inputs.

Nekovar defines the cup product on the tame small complex by transporting the continuous-cochain cup product through its comparison maps. Section7.5.1 gives the explicit components; Sections7.5.3–7.5.5 identify the local trace and perfect local duality.

Nekovar Sections8.1.4–8.1.6 give integral cochain corestriction, compatibility through towers, its trace description up to homotopy, and cup/trace compatibility for the abelian quotients used there.

Nekovar Sections7.4.7–7.4.9 give the projective profinite small resolution, its comparison to the bar resolution, a functorial homotopy from the composite on continuous cochains to the identity, and the stated compatible second homotopy.

Milne, Arithmetic Duality Theorems, I (1.1b) and Example1.6(b), identifies the local full H2 invariant with Q/Z and states that restriction multiplies the invariant by the extension degree. Nekovar5.1.3,5.2.1 and7.5.3 give the finite/adic invariant and its tame normalization. Corestriction composed with restriction equals the subgroup index by the coefficient trace: the restricted class corresponds to the constant induced tuple, whose trace sums one identical entry for each coset.
