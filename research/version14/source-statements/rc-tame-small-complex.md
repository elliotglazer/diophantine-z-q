# Tame local small complex

This card paraphrases the retained published source and preserves its geometric Frobenius convention.

For residue characteristic distinct from the coefficient prime, wild-inertia invariants compute the continuous local cohomology. For a tame module, choose inertia generator t and geometric Frobenius f with t f=f t^q. Nekovar defines the three-term complex with differentials (f-1,t-1) and (1-t,theta-1), where theta=f(1+t+...+t^(q-1)).

Propositions 7.2.3 and 7.2.4 give functorial quasi-isomorphisms to and from continuous cochains, with the small-complex composite equal to the identity. Section 7.2.2 supplies the procyclic two-term model and coefficient-prime cohomological dimension one.

Source: J. Nekovar, Selmer Complexes, Asterisque 310 (2006), Sections 7.1.1 and 7.2.1–7.2.4, printed pages 155–160. Retained PDF SHA256 61c84e5ad3252a2e520747215ac57a282addc2b58c824a3637bcd77bbe02153f.
