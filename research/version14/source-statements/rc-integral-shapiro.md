# Integral Shapiro and corestriction

Source: Jan Nekovar, Selmer Complexes, Asterisque310 (2006), Sections8.1.1–8.1.6, printed189–194, especially8.1.4 on191.

For an open subgroup U of a profinite group G and discrete continuous modules, the Shapiro evaluation map from induced continuous cochains to subgroup cochains is a quasi-isomorphism. For a discrete G-module, the finite coset-sum corestriction is a cochain map. Its homotopy class is independent of the section; the source gives an explicit integral section-change homotopy. Compatible coset representatives for a tower make the cochain transfers compose. The constructions contain no division by the index.

Boundary: finite coefficient modules and open subgroups are the source setting used here. Writing right-coset formulas with left representatives and inserting the actual character-twisted G-action are the existing consumer construction. No infinite character, normalized averaging or automatic compatibility of arbitrary separately chosen sections is asserted.
