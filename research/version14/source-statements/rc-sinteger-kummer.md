# S-integer Kummer inputs

This card paraphrases published source inputs, not the original text.

For an open arithmetic scheme U=Spec O_(K,S), H0(U,Gm) is O_(K,S)* and H1(U,Gm) is Pic(U). If n is invertible on U, the etale Kummer sequence 1 to mu_n to Gm to Gm to 1 is exact. For locally constant finite coefficients whose order is invertible on U, etale cohomology agrees with cohomology of the Galois group unramified outside S.

Source: J. S. Milne, Arithmetic Duality Theorems, second edition (2006), II Proposition 2.1 and proof pp163–164, Proposition 2.9 and its Kummer-sequence proof pp170–171. Retained PDF SHA256 2c6195ec76a974f3f336c77cb71cc3845b018aad2d43136716b3477a3bc5fb31.
