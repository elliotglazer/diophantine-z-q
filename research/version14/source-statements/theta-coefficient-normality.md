# Normality of the coefficient ring

Source declarations: The Stacks Project, Lemma10.37.6 Tag00GZ (a PID is normal), Lemma10.37.9 Tag0BI0 (if R is Noetherian normal, R[[t]] is Noetherian normal), and Lemma10.37.5 Tag00GY (a localization of a normal domain is normal). Apply these to Z2, whose nonzero ideals are generated by the least2-adic valuation occurring in them. Thus Z2 is a Noetherian PID and normal. The power-series statement gives Lambda=Z2[[t]] Noetherian normal; localization gives A=Lambda[1/2] normal. Localization is Noetherian because every localized ideal is the extension of its contraction, which is finitely generated. This supplies the actual normal-domain premise at main14174–14178. It does not supply the separate height-one valuation inequalities or the fixed denominator estimate.
