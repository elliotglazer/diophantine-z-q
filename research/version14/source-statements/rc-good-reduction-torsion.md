# Good reduction and prime-to-residue torsion

This card paraphrases the published source.

Reduction on a smooth Weierstrass model respects the elliptic group law. On torsion of order prime to the residue characteristic it is injective. If the elliptic curve has good reduction, that torsion and the corresponding prime-adic Tate module are unramified. Reduction commutes with the Galois action.
