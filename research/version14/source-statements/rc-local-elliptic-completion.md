# Local elliptic group structure

This is a source paraphrase, not the original publication.

For an elliptic curve E over a finite extension F of Q_p, E(F) contains a subgroup of finite index isomorphic to the additive group of the ring of integers of F.

Source: J. H. Silverman, The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves, second edition (2009), VII Proposition 6.3 and its proof, printed page 200.
