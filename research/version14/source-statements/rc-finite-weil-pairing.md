# Finite and Tate Weil pairing

Source: J. H. Silverman, The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves, second edition (2009), III Section 8, Proposition 8.1(a)–(e), printed94, and Proposition8.3 with preceding inverse-limit construction, printed97–98.

For an elliptic curve E over K and m>=2 prime to char(K), the finite Weil pairing E[m] x E[m] -> mu_m is bilinear, alternating, nondegenerate and Galois equivariant, and satisfies the stated compatibility under multiplication maps. For a prime l distinct from char(K), the compatible pairings induce the bilinear alternating nondegenerate Galois-equivariant Tate pairing T_l(E) x T_l(E) -> Z_l(1).

Source boundary: the published proof erratum for Proposition8.1(b) replaces the intermediate identity by G^m=F composed with [m], which still proves constancy and alternation. The generalized-isogeny pairing remark is not used. Twisting coefficient modules, scalar extension, conjugation cancellation and Selmer duality are consumer arguments, not additional statements attributed to this source.
