+
+                                             10 November 2026

+                                                              Abstract
+             Under the specified modular-realization, elliptic, pole-realization and prime-pattern
+        inputs of the source construction, we give an integer quartic family in 238 total variables
+        whose sufficiently large members define the integers in the rationals. A proved contact-
+        height estimate with exponent 7/2 becomes 7/4 after using two disjoint contact blocks. A
+        quadratic chart in four field coordinates stores the common point and both reciprocal formal
+        parameters. All elliptic comparison ratios use the plain formal parameter. Sixteen local
+        tests are represented by five factors and three scalar weight outputs. A balanced contact
+        normalization removes a separate reciprocal coordinate, while a fourth-power scalar norm
+        certificate bounds the input height by an arbitrarily small fixed multiple of c(1 + log c),
+        where c is an auxiliary integer index. A sharpened Lucas estimate then supplies the required
+        contradiction. The quartic is a sum of 212 primitive quadratic squares, has 2924 monomials,
+        and has coefficient height 1494N+2 + 1 for N ≥ 53. The sufficient defining parameter remains
+        ineffective because of the additive constant in the aggregate trace-height theorem. No abc
+        conjecture is added.

+A Quartic in Ninety-Three Variables under the Rational abc
+                              Conjecture
+
+                    10 November 2026
+
+                                                    Abstract
+
+    Assuming the rational abc conjecture and the specified elliptic-group, pole-realization
+and simultaneous prime-pattern inputs, we construct a family of integer quartics in
+ninety-three total variables. Every sufficiently large fixed odd input shift gives rational
+input projection exactly Z. The quartic is a sum of eighty-one primitive quadratic
+squares and has exactly 920 monomials. Its coefficient height, for a shift B ≥ 3, is
+max(4153456, B2 + 797687). A common elliptic chart in four field coordinates stores both
+reciprocal formal parameters. Eight rational contacts symmetric about one permit a short
+quadratic evaluation and normalization. A height estimate for a sum of fourth powers
+bounds the input together with one elliptic index. All six local tests have order one; only
+plain elliptic formal parameters are used. The sufficient shift is ineffective.
+
+1 Statement and arithmetic assumptions
+
+For a reduced fraction u/v, with v > 0, put h(u/v) = log max(|u|, v). Write h(1 : u : v) for
+the logarithmic projective height of the indicated rational point. The coefficient height of
+an integer polynomial is the maximum absolute value of its coefficients. All variable counts
+include the distinguished input.
+
+Theorem 1.1. Under the assumptions below, there is a computable family
+
+                    PB ∈ Z[X, Y1, . . . , Y92], B ≥ 3 odd,
+
+such that every member has degree exactly four, content one, exactly 920 monomials, and
+
