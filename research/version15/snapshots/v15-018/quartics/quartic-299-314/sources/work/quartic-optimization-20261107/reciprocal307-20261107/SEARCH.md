# Earlier x-only routes and the retained repair

The initial search asked whether deleting elliptic y-type coordinates could save several field pairs. A pure division-polynomial x-chain does not, by itself, establish rational lifts on E. The same x formulas hold on quadratic twists. Even a rational square x need not lift: x=9 has x^3-Dx=630+54rho, whose norm391068 is3 modulo5 and is not a rational square. A square in K would have square norm. This is a local obstruction to that raw replacement, not a counterexample to a complete candidate.

A known rational non-two-torsion translation point can remove a lift ambiguity: x(U) and x(U-P) in the corresponding Kummer relation determine y(U) through a linear numerator divided by2y(P). The accepted H presentation already exploits related recovery identities. In the other blocks the translation roots vary, so their ordinates are variables and cannot be counted as coefficients. Directly importing a fixed-P degree count would be incorrect.

The productive representation used actual reciprocal points f=1/x,h=-y/x^2,Y=f^2. Its curve h^2=f-DfY is quadratic. The first oriented abscissa attempt introduced Z=fF and imposed

    c*(Y+Yalt-2Z)=(f+F)*(1-DZ)+2hk.

At F=f,k=-h this vanishes for every c. The true difference needs the tangent condition. The second usual identity

    c*((f+F)*(1-DZ)-2hk)=(1+DZ)^2

repairs the tangent but is cubic in the stored variables. It was not counted as a quadratic residual.

The successful repair replaces Z by a slope s. The symmetric divided-difference equation s(h-k)=1-D(Y+fF+Yalt), together with s(f-F)=h+k and s^2=c-D(f+F), supplies the tangent condition with no new pair. The first search also noticed that hL+fH=0 and kz=F leave ratios free at O. The additional regular equations in the frozen candidate resolve those endpoints. These corrections are part of the frozen307 equations, not unresolved exceptions.

Deleting the root square-root a as well was considered but is not used. The current argument restores b=yR/a and then consumes the old root squareclass and integer-label results. Dropping a would need a separate root-domain and height proof. It is not justified by the accepted auxiliary-domain extension.
