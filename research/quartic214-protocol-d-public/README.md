# Review records for the 214-variable existence family

This directory records the source-first review and public-source integration of [the 214-variable construction](../quartic214/README.md). The polynomial has 214 total variables, including its input. Its defining assertion is for every parameter above one fixed **ineffective** threshold. The coefficient estimate at N>=53 does not certify a particular integer-defining member.

The initial independent reconstruction is preserved under `initial/`. Current source-use reconciliation, application interfaces, direct dependencies and findings are recorded separately from reviewer-authored decisions. A component decision covers its stated scope only. The assembled-proof and coverage decisions must be read with their exact frozen sources and limitations.

The governing standard is the repository's [Protocol D](../../docs/research-review/PROTOCOL.md). The public [Astra acceptance policy](../../docs/research-review/ASTRA-REVIEW-POLICY.md) additionally requires trusted controller records and the designated App check on the actual PR revision. Contributor-supplied model names and review files cannot authorize that check. A local record check establishes its documented finite relationships; it does not prove the arithmetic or authenticate the serving model.

This is a research contribution, not a new numbered manuscript release. Version 15's manuscript, PDF and certified coefficient statements retain their existing identity. The new construction is supplied as complete quadratic rows, a symbolic coefficient table, reproducible exact algebra and linked mathematical proofs. The project profile explains the publication and handoff scope.
