V04: tau chart remains research, with a further trace-unit concern
----------------------------------------------------------------
The supplied notes leave proxy41, guard completeness and anchor obligations
open. There is also a split-prime sign issue to settle before a proxy unit
can be asserted. The existing source proves w*k_Q=-h_P mod p without
choosing signs; division by h_S changes the residue to -h_P/h_S. From
h_S^2=h_P^2 mod p alone one gets plus or minus one independently at the
two split places, not necessarily the same sign.

For example at the admitted split prime23, take sqrt2 residues +/-5.
The fixed h_P=-1+7*sqrt2/4 has residues (2,19). Allowed square-root residues
(2,4) for h_S then give -h_P/h_S=(-1,+1), whose rational trace-half is zero.
This is a local sign diagnostic, NOT a constructed global false solution
of the proposed circuit. It demonstrates that the stated local hypotheses
alone do not justify the needed trace-unit inference. A global branch or
index argument could potentially rule it out; none was supplied or checked.
Do not restrict the problem to the nonunit place over41, and do not carry
forward the old proxy acceptance. A and tau do not automatically combine:
A retains the w-anchor; tau changes that object. Keep tau out of the first
three PRs until these obligations are closed.
