This blueprint formalizes Theorem 1.1 of the 11/5 manuscript *ℤ is Diophantine in ℚ*
(`diophantine_zq.tex` in this repository), with its deep arithmetic inputs as explicit
hypotheses and its finite computations checked by the Lean kernel.
