# The index $[E(K_0):2E(K_0)]=8$

$2E(K_0)$ is the kernel of the Kummer map $\alpha:E(K_0)\to K_0^\times/K_0^{\times2}$, and
$$[E(K_0):2E(K_0)]=8 .$$
More precisely $\alpha$ induces an isomorphism of $E(K_0)/2E(K_0)$ onto the subgroup of
$K_0^\times/K_0^{\times2}$ generated by $-1,\epsilon,d$, which is elementary abelian of order $8$.

**Proof sketch.** The manuscript uses the exact two-isogeny sequence
$$0\to E'(K_0)[\hat\varphi]/\varphi(E(K_0)[2])\to E'(K_0)/\varphi(E(K_0))\to E(K_0)/2E(K_0)
\to E(K_0)/\hat\varphi(E'(K_0))\to0,$$
whose outer terms are the two Kummer images; the first term vanishes because the nonkernel
two-torsion points $(\pm d,0)$ map to the generator $(0,0)$ of the dual kernel. A direct route
with the same inputs: since the second descent image is trivial, the kernel theorem for
$\varphi=\varphi_{-d^2}$ gives $\varphi(E(K_0))=E'(K_0)$. The kernel theorem for
$\varphi_{4d^2}:E'\to E_{-16d^2}$, transported by the rescaling $\lambda_{-d^2}$ (which multiplies
abscissas by $1/4$, a square, and sends the class $-16d^2$ of $(0,0)$ to $-d^2$), gives
$\ker\alpha=\hat\varphi(E'(K_0))$. Since $\hat\varphi\circ\varphi=[2]$,
$$2E(K_0)=\hat\varphi\bigl(\varphi(E(K_0))\bigr)=\hat\varphi(E'(K_0))=\ker\alpha .$$
As $\alpha$ is a homomorphism, $E(K_0)/2E(K_0)\cong\alpha(E(K_0))$, of order $8$ by the first
descent image.
