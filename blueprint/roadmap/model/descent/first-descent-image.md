# The first descent image has order eight

Let $\alpha=\alpha_{-d^2}:E(K_0)\to K_0^\times/K_0^{\times2}$ be the Kummer map of $E$. Its image
is the subgroup generated by the classes of $-1$, $\epsilon=1+s$ and $d$; this subgroup has
order $8$, its elements being the classes of $\pm1,\pm\epsilon,\pm d,\pm\epsilon d$, which are
pairwise distinct. The two-torsion points give
$$\alpha(O)=1,\quad\alpha((0,0))=-d^2\equiv-1,\quad\alpha((d,0))=d,\quad\alpha((-d,0))=-d,$$
and the point $(\epsilon^{-4}(1+s),\,\epsilon^{-6}\cdot2s)\in E(K_0)$, the preimage of
$R=(1+s,2s)\in C(K_0)$ under the change of variables, has class $\epsilon$.

**Proof sketch.** *Upper bound.* Let $(x,y)\in E(K_0)$ with $x\ne0$ and let $\pi$ be a prime of
$\mathbb Z[s]$ not associate to $d$; put $m=v_\pi(x)$. If $y=0$ then $x=\pm d$ and $m=0$.
Otherwise $2v_\pi(y)=v_\pi(x^3-d^2x)$, where the two terms have valuations $3m$ and
$m+2v_\pi(d)=m$; if $m\ne0$ these differ, so $2v_\pi(y)=\min(3m,m)$, which is odd when $m$ is
odd. Hence every $v_\pi(x)$ with $\pi\not\sim d$ is even, and by unique factorization
$x=u\,d^{e}z^2$ with $u$ a unit, $e\in\{0,1\}$, $z\in K_0^\times$; the unit classes are those of
$\pm1,\pm\epsilon$. The class of $(0,0)$ is $-d^2\equiv-1$. *Distinctness.* $v_d$ is odd on
$\pm d,\pm\epsilon d$ and even on $\pm1,\pm\epsilon$; $d$ is totally positive, so within each
half the four sign vectors $(+,+),(-,-),(+,-),(-,+)$ separate the classes, and squares are
totally positive. *Lower bound.* The listed two-torsion values give $-1$ and $d$. The point
$R$ lies on $C$ and the change of variables maps $E$ isomorphically onto $C$ scaling
abscissas by $\epsilon^4$, so its preimage lies on $E$ with abscissa
$\epsilon^{-4}(1+s)\equiv\epsilon$. Since $\alpha$ is a homomorphism, its image contains the
subgroup generated by $-1,\epsilon,d$, hence equals it.
