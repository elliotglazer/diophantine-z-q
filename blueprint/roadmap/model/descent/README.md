# Descent, torsion and rank

The classical two-isogeny descent for curves $y^2=x^3+bx$ supplies the Kummer map, its
multiplicativity, the identification of its kernel with the image of an isogeny, and the
factorization of doubling through the isogeny and its dual.

## The descent index

The point $R=(1+s,2s)$ lives on the isomorphic curve $C:y^2=x^3-\delta^2x$, $\delta=3-s$, and its
doubling, transported back, is $P_0$. On $E$ the Kummer image is generated by the classes of
$-1$, $1+s$ and $d$, of order eight; on the isogenous curve $E':y^2=x^3+4d^2x$ it is trivial.
Together these give the weak Mordell-Weil index.

## Torsion and rank

Reduction at the primes $(17,s-6)$ and $(23,s-5)$, with $20$ and $24$ points, shows that the
torsion is exactly the full two-torsion; in particular $P_0$ has infinite order, so its nonzero
multiples are affine with nonzero coordinates. The Mordell-Weil theorem then gives rank one, and
real signs of squareclasses show that $R$ is an odd multiple of a generator modulo torsion.
