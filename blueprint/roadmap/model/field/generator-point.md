# The point $P_0$

The point $P_0\in E(K_0)$ is the affine point
$$P_0=\left(\frac{1641-1160s}{8},\ \frac{-123720+87483s}{32}\right)
=\left(\tfrac{1641}{8}-145s,\ -\tfrac{15465}{4}+\tfrac{87483}{32}s\right).$$
Its four rational coordinates are $\frac{1641}{8},-145,-\frac{15465}{4},\frac{87483}{32}$.

That these coordinates satisfy the equation of $E$ is a closed finite identity in $K_0$,
proved separately. Proposition 3.1 shows that $P_0$ has infinite order and generates
$2E(K_0)$.
