# The $2$-adic valuations of the coordinates of $P_0$

$$v_2(1641)=0,\qquad v_2(1160)=3,\qquad v_2(123720)=3,\qquad v_2(87483)=0,\qquad
v_2(8)=3,\qquad v_2(32)=5 .$$
Equivalently: $1641$, $87483$, $145$ and $15465$ are odd, $1160=2^3\cdot145$ and
$123720=2^3\cdot15465$.

This is a finite computation with numbers below $2\cdot10^5$. It is the numerical input of the
statement that $P_0=\bigl((1641-1160s)/8,\,(-123720+87483s)/32\bigr)$ lies in the formal group
at the prime $(s)$.
