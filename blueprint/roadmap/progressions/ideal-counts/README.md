# Ideal counts in quadratic fields

The Dedekind zeta function is the Dirichlet series of the number $a_K(n)$ of ideals of norm
$n$. These counts are multiplicative. In a quadratic field, at a prime power $p^k$ they are
the partial sums of the powers of a sign $\varepsilon_p\in\{-1,0,1\}$, and if
$\mathcal O_K$ contains a square root of a non-square integer $d$, then for odd $p\nmid d$
that sign is the Legendre symbol $(d/p)$. Comparing Euler products then factors $\zeta_K$ as
$\zeta$ times an $L$-function, up to finitely many local factors.
