# Jacobian formulas on the weighted model

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $A\in R$; write $\mathcal E(X,Y,Z)=Y^2-X^3-AXZ^4$. Define
the Jacobian doubling and addition
$$\mathrm{dbl}(X,Y,Z)=(M^2-2S,\ M(S-X_3)-8Y^4,\ 2YZ),\quad M=3X^2+AZ^4,\ S=4XY^2,\ X_3=M^2-2S,$$
and, for $P_1=(X_1,Y_1,Z_1)$, $P_2=(X_2,Y_2,Z_2)$, with $U_1=X_1Z_2^2$, $U_2=X_2Z_1^2$,
$S_1=Y_1Z_2^3$, $S_2=Y_2Z_1^3$, $H=U_2-U_1$, $\Lambda=S_2-S_1$,
$$\mathrm{add}(P_1,P_2)=\bigl(\Lambda^2-H^3-2U_1H^2,\ \Lambda(U_1H^2-X_3)-S_1H^3,\ HZ_1Z_2\bigr),$$
$X_3$ being the first coordinate. Then the following polynomial identities hold:

1. $\mathcal E(\mathrm{dbl}(X,Y,Z))$ lies in the ideal generated by $\mathcal E(X,Y,Z)$;
2. $\mathcal E(\mathrm{add}(P_1,P_2))$ lies in the ideal generated by $\mathcal E(P_1)$ and
   $\mathcal E(P_2)$;
3. for doubling over a field of characteristic $\ne2$ with $YZ\ne0$, with $x=X/Z^2$,
   $y=Y/Z^3$ and $\lambda=(3x^2+A)/(2y)=M/Z_3$ where $Z_3=2YZ$: $X_3/Z_3^2=\lambda^2-2x$, by the identity
   $X_3=M^2-2xZ_3^2$ (as $xZ_3^2=4XY^2=S$);
4. $Y_3/Z_3^3=\lambda(x-X_3/Z_3^2)-y$ for doubling, by the identity
   $Y_3=M(XZ_3^2/Z^2-X_3)-8Y^4$ with $Z_3^2/Z^2=4Y^2$;
5. for addition over a field with $Z_1Z_2H\ne0$, with $x_i=X_i/Z_i^2$, $y_i=Y_i/Z_i^3$ and
   $\lambda=(y_2-y_1)/(x_2-x_1)=\Lambda/Z_3$: $X_3/Z_3^2=\lambda^2-x_1-x_2$, by the identity
   $X_3=\Lambda^2-H^2(U_1+U_2)$ and $(x_1+x_2)Z_3^2=(U_1+U_2)H^2$;
6. $Y_3/Z_3^3=\lambda(x_1-X_3/Z_3^2)-y_1$ for addition, by $x_1Z_3^2=U_1H^2$ and
   $y_1Z_3^3=S_1H^3$;
7. (equal abscissas) over a field with $Z_1Z_2\ne0$: $H=0\iff x_1=x_2$, and then
   $\Lambda=0\iff y_1=y_2$; for points of the curve over a field of characteristic $\ne2$, the
   sum is the doubling if $\Lambda=0$ and the identity $O$ otherwise (opposite points);
8. (zero ordinate) over a field of characteristic $\ne2$, if $Y=0$ and $Z\ne0$ the point has
   order two and its double is $O$; negation is $(X,Y,Z)\mapsto(X,-Y,Z)$.

Together these say that the Jacobian formulas, with the branches 7 and 8 and $Z=0$ for $O$,
compute the chord-and-tangent law of $E$ on representatives. They are polynomial
identities, independent of the generator theorem. That the fixed projective point is the
affine $P_0$ is the separate finite check on the fixed Jacobian point.

The manuscript reports nine polynomial identities for the nonexceptional projective
formulas and the preservation of $Y^2=X^3+AXZ^4$ without listing them; the list above is
the set this chapter uses.

**Proof sketch.** Items 1-6 are identities in polynomial rings over $\mathbb Z$,
checked by ring normalization (items 1 and 2 with explicit cofactors, found by polynomial
division by $\mathcal E$). Item 7: $U_1=U_2$ iff $x_1=x_2$ after dividing by $Z_1^2Z_2^2$, and
likewise for $S_i$; on the curve, equal abscissas give $y_2=\pm y_1$. Item 8 is
$-(x,0)=(x,0)$.
