# The density statement for good slope primes

Let $G$ be the set of good slope primes. The *density statement* is the assertion that $G$ has
positive relative upper density in the primes: there is $c>0$ such that
$$\#\bigl(G\cap[0,N]\bigr)\ge c\,\pi(N)\qquad\text{for infinitely many }N,$$
where $\pi(N)$ is the number of primes at most $N$.

This is the weak form of Proposition D of the route AP note; numerically the relative density of
$G$ is about $0.106$. Together with Szemerédi's theorem in the primes it gives long arithmetic
progressions of good slope primes. It is proved in this chapter
([Proposition D](proposition-d.md)).
