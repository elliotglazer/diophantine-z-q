# Bounds for the fixed constants

Only a few small facts are computed: the size of the primorial, the factorial $324!$ to
within its binary length, and $G_0<2^{37}$.

- [Small explicit bounds](small-constant-bounds.md)

The symbolic estimates of the whole chapter also use a handful of closed comparisons between
explicit numbers of at most a few thousand binary digits; they are collected in one place.

- [Closed numerical comparisons](closed-comparisons.md)

The catalogue bound puts every catalogue prime below $X=2^{W^8}$. This bounds the catalogue
maximum and product, hence $N_s$ and the pole clearing constant $\ell$.

- [The catalogue below the progression cutoff](catalogue-bounds.md)
- [Size of $N_s$ and $\ell$](catalogue-size.md)

The least formal multiple is at most $N_s^{10}$ (proved in the next section), so
$c_*=4M_0$ is below $2^{2^{4617}}$, far below $D$, whose double logarithm lies between
$8008$ and $9010$.

- [Size of the formal multiple constant](formal-multiple-size.md)
- [Bounds for $D$](d-bounds.md)

Hence $D$ dominates the other entries of $R$, and with $R=D$ the maximum defining
$A_{\rm ht}$ is attained by $Q^{17}$. This identifies $T$ with a closed expression in $D$ and
$G_0$, which is bounded by monotonicity alone.
