# Hecke's continuation of $L(C_d,s)$

*Hecke's continuation* is the following assertion. For every natural number $d\ne0$, $L(C_d,s)$
has an entire continuation.

The curve $C_d$ has complex multiplication by $\mathbb Z[i]$, so $L(C_d,s)$ is the L-function of a
Hecke character of $\mathbb Q(i)$ (Deuring, Hecke), and that L-function is entire. It is a
hypothesis, not proved here.
