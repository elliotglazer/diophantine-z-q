# Tian–Yuan–Zhang's Theorem 3.5(2) in reading A

*Theorem 3.5(2) in reading A* is the following assertion. For every natural number $d$ with
$d\equiv5\pmod8$ and $d$ squarefree, and every entire continuation $F$ of $L(C_d,s)$ whose order
of vanishing at $s=1$ is at least $2$, both genus sums are even:
$$T^{\rm Cl}_{5a}(d)=0\quad\text{and}\quad T^{\rm Cl}_{5b}(d)=0\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z/2.$$

This is Theorem 3.5(2) of Tian, Yuan and Zhang for $n\equiv5\pmod8$, in reading A (where $d_1=1$
is admitted), with their hypothesis that $2^{-\rho(n)}\mathcal L(n)$ is even replaced by the
stronger hypothesis $\mathcal L(n)=0$, which they identify with order of vanishing at least two;
so the assertion is weaker than their theorem. Proposition N (first sentence), an unpublished
argument that has not been refereed or checked by a human reader, derives it from their
unambiguous statements; their printed Theorem 1.2 is not cited. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
