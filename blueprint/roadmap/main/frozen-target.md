# The target statement

There is a polynomial $P\in\mathbb Z[X_0,\ldots,X_{292890231904}]$ of total degree less
than $2\uparrow\uparrow 7$, every coefficient of which has absolute value less than
$2\uparrow\uparrow 8$, such that for every $q\in\mathbb Q$,
$$q\in\mathbb Z\iff\exists\,w\in\mathbb Q^{292890231904},\ P(q,w)=0.$$

Here $2\uparrow\uparrow 1=2$ and $2\uparrow\uparrow(n+1)=2^{2\uparrow\uparrow n}$.
