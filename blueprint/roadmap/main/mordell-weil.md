# Mordell–Weil for the generator curve

The group $E(K_0)$ of $K_0$-rational points of $E:y^2=x^3-d^2x$, with $K_0=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$
and $d=75-53\sqrt2$, is finitely generated.

This is the Mordell–Weil theorem for $E/K_0$, used in the generator argument to pass from
the descent index $[E(K_0):2E(K_0)]=8$ and the torsion subgroup to rank one. It is proved in
[Mordell–Weil from the descent](../model/descent/mordell-weil-from-descent.md), so it is no
longer a hypothesis of the main theorem.
