# The ambiguous classes fill $\mathrm{Cl}[2]$

Let $n$ be odd and squarefree and $\mathrm{Cl}$ the class group of the ring of integers of
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-n})$. Then the image of $\psi$ is the 2-torsion subgroup:
$$\psi\bigl(\mathbb F_2^{T_n}\bigr)=\mathrm{Cl}[2]=\{c\in\mathrm{Cl}: c^2=1\}.$$

**Proof sketch.** The values of $\psi$ have square $1$. Conversely, let $c=[I]$ with $I\ne0$ and
$c^2=1$. Since $I\cdot\sigma I=(N(I))$ is principal, $[\sigma I]=c^{-1}=c$. So
$(x)\,\sigma I=(y)\,I$ for some nonzero $x,y\in\mathcal O$. Hilbert 90 gives $\nu\ne0$ with
$(\nu)I$ invariant under $\sigma$, so its class, which is $c$, lies in the image of $\psi$.
