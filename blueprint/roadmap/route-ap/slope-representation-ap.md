# The slope representation under field 2′

The *slope representation under field 2′* is the assertion that every integer $A$ is a sum
$t_1+t_2+t_3=A$ of three rationals each satisfying conditions 1, 2′, 3, 4 for $B_0=0$.

It is weaker than the slope representation, which it replaces in the completeness of the
multiplication template.
