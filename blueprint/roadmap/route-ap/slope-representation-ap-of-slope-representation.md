# The slope representation implies its form under field 2′

The slope representation implies the slope representation under field 2′.

**Proof sketch.** A good slope satisfies condition 2 at every $p\in S_b$, hence the first
alternative of field 2′; conditions 1, 3 and 4 are unchanged.

So everything the twenty-two-prime pattern or the two-slope pattern gives, route AP accepts as
well.
