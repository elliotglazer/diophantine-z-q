## 1. The modular-curve part: `X0RatStructure ⇐ X0ModuliGalois` (no CM)

`X0RatStructure` (tyz-split): for `g` with `IsRatModFn N g` and `τ, τ'`, if the moduli coordinates
`modCoord N τ i` (`i = 0`: `j(τ)`; `1 ≤ i < N`: elementary symmetric functions of the Weber values
`h(k/N)`, `1 ≤ k < N`) are algebraic and `ρ ∈ Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)` maps them to those of `τ'`, then `g(τ), g(τ')` are
algebraic and `ρ`-conjugate.

**Leaf `X0ModuliGalois`.** For `N ≥ 1`, `g` with `IsRatModFn N g`, `σ : ℂ ≃+* ℂ`, `τ, τ'`, `μ`: if the
moduli coordinates of `τ` are algebraic, `σ(g₂(τ)) = μ⁴g₂(τ')`, `σ(g₃(τ)) = μ⁶g₃(τ')` (`IsConjModel`:
`(x, y) ↦ (μ⁻²x, μ⁻³y)` is an isomorphism `E_τ^σ ≅ E_τ'`), and (for `N ≥ 2`) for some `1 ≤ k < N`,
`σ(℘_τ(1/N)) = μ²℘_τ'(k/N)`, `σ(℘'_τ(1/N)) = μ³℘'_τ'(k/N)`, then `σ(g(τ)) = g(τ')`.
Why it is a consequence of the print, never stronger: the hypothesis says that the isomorphism sends `σ(P)`,
`P = ξ_τ(1/N)` the generator of `C_τ = ⟨1/N⟩`, to `ξ_τ'(k/N)`; since `σ(P)` has exact order `N`, so does
`k/N`, hence `(k, N) = 1` and `(E_τ, C_τ)^σ ≅ (E_τ', C_τ')`; the conclusion is the Galois equivariance
`g(x)^σ = g(x^σ)` of the canonical model `X₀(N)_ℚ` (the model whose function field is the field of
functions with rational `q`-expansion at `∞`, the cusp `∞` being the Tate curve with `μ_N`, which matches
`⟨1/N⟩`) at the `ℚ̄`-point `x = (E_τ, C_τ)`. The algebraicity hypothesis was added after the first review
(it makes the leaf the `ℚ̄`-point form; both uses supply it).

### 2.4 The remaining glue `RingClassLattice`

```lean
def RingClassLattice : Prop :=
  ∀ (m f : ℕ) [NeZero m] [NeZero f], Squarefree m →
  ∀ (J : integralIdealsPrimeTo (conductorOrder (QNeg m) f).conductorModulus)
    (s : IdeleGroup (𝓞 (QNeg m)) (QNeg m)),
    ringClassIdeleQuotient (conductorOrder (QNeg m) f) (icl s) =
      (conductorOrder (QNeg m) f).picClass J →
    ∃ k : QNeg m, k ≠ 0 ∧ idealOf m f s = (fun x => k * x) '' idealMeetOrder m f J
```

(`idealOf m f s = {j ∈ K : s_f⁻¹ j ∈ Ô_O}`, `Ô_O` the closure of `O` in the finite adeles;
`idealMeetOrder m f J = J ∩ O`.) Sanity checks: for the idele `π_𝔭` of a prime `𝔭 ∤ f`, Tau Ceti gives the
class of `𝔭` and `idealOf = 𝔭 ∩ O` (`k = 1`); principal ideles multiply both sides by `k`; ideles with finite
part in `Ô_O^×` change neither side. Proof route: move `s` by a principal idele into the congruence subgroup
of the conductor (Tau Ceti `exists_unitEmbedding_mul_mem_ideleCongrOneSubgroup`); its ray class is that of
`I = toFractionalIdeal(s_f)` (`rayClassQuotient_mk_of_mem`); the hypothesis and `mem_ker_rayClassToPic` give
`I = βγ·J` with `β ∈ O` and `γ ≡ 1 mod* 𝔣`; membership `j ∈ I ⇔ s_f⁻¹ j ∈ 𝒪̂` (via Tau Ceti's
`toFractionalIdeal_le_one_iff` and multiplicativity), `Ô_O = {z ∈ 𝒪̂ : ∃ a ∈ ℤ, z − a ∈ f𝒪̂}`, and the
congruence conditions at the primes above `f` give `idealOf s = βγ·(J ∩ O)`. Estimated 10–15 hours of Lean
