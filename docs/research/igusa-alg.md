## 2. Method: one Kronecker evaluation in the kernel instead of `ring`

theta-glue found that `ring`, `grind`, a dynamic program, a split proof and list-based reflection all exceed
the memory or time budget for degrees 40-72. Two findings here made the problem small.

**(a) Halving the degree** (`py/sforms.py`, VERIFIED, exact, sympy):
* Group the ten `θ_m⁸ = t_m⁴` into the four `t_{(00,b)}⁴` and the three pairs `{(01,00),(01,10)}`,
  `{(10,00),(10,01)}` and `{(11,00),(11,11)}`. For each pair, `y+y'` and `yy'` are polynomials in
  `s_i = U_i²`. The odd terms in `P = U₀U₁U₂U₃` cancel inside each pair.
* `4ψ₄`, `4ψ₆`, `∏θ_m² = −2¹⁴χ₁₀` and `2¹⁷·3·χ₁₂` are polynomials in `s`, with 11, 24, 48 and 85 terms.
* So `IgusaAlg k` becomes an integer identity of degree `4k` in `s`:
  `D_k · E_k(blocks) = Σ n·Ψ₄^i Ψ₆^j X₁₀^l X₁₂^m`, where `D_k = 432, 6912, 3456, 186624, 5184, 1728, 216` for
  `k = 3..9`. For example, `216 e₉ = X₁₂³ + 112 Ψ₆X₁₀³ − 30 Ψ₄X₁₀²X₁₂`.
* All seven identities were checked by full expansion.

**(b) One evaluation decides the identity** (`Core.lean`, namespace `IgusaAlgEval`, VERIFIED).
`Data.gen_eq_zero` states that if:
* the blocks are syntactically homogeneous of degrees 4 and 8, and the right side of degree `4k`
  (`PE.hd`, checked by `decide +kernel`);
* the majorant (all coefficients replaced by their absolute values) at `(1,1,1,1)` is `< N`;
* the integer value at `(1, N, N^D, N^{D²})` with `D = 4k+1` is `0`;

then the identity holds in every commutative ring. The proof has four steps:
1. The polynomial `P ∈ ℤ[X₀..X₃]` is homogeneous of degree `4k` (Mathlib's `IsHomogeneous`).
2. The Kronecker map `X₀,X₁,X₂,X₃ ↦ 1, T, T^D, T^{D²}` is injective on such polynomials (`kron_eq_zero`,
   base-`D` digits).
3. The image `H ∈ ℤ[T]` has every coefficient bounded by the majorant (`Maj`, closed under `+`, `−`, `·`,
   powers).
4. An integer polynomial whose coefficients have absolute value `≤ M < N` and with `H(N) = 0` is zero
   (`eq_zero_of_eval_eq_zero_of_coeff_lt`, by induction with `divX`).

The kernel evaluates the left side with GMP arithmetic. The values have about 3.8·10⁶ bits for `k = 9`, with
`N = 2⁷⁵`, and the check takes about 2 s.

Evidence that the kernel really computes (VERIFIED): changing `D₉` from 216 to 217 makes the check fail with
a kernel type mismatch. A grid-interpolation alternative (2000 points) hit a kernel deterministic timeout at
38 GB, so a single Kronecker point is the essential choice.

**(c) Bridge to theta-glue's statement** (section `IgusaAlgKron`, VERIFIED):
* `esymmL_blocks` regroups the ten-element `esymmL` into the seven blocks.
* `blocks_eval`, `psi4T_eq`, `psi6T_eq`, `chi10T_eq` and `chi12T_eq` match `Sq m u` with the `s`-forms. Each is
  a `ring` of degree ≤ 24 in `U`.
* `rhsK_eq` is a `ring` over the atoms `Ψ₄, Ψ₆, X₁₀, X₁₂`.
* `igusaAlg_of_data` cancels `D_k`.

* In theta-glue's integration plan (theta-glue §7), the GLUE field `∀ k, 5 ≤ k → k ≤ 9 → IgusaAlg k` disappears.

  ThetaGlue code. `Data.gen_eq_zero` is reusable for any big homogeneous integer identity in four variables.
  For another variable count, `kron` and `digits3` need a variant.

  ≤ 24) and to theta-glue's and theta-split's own code.
