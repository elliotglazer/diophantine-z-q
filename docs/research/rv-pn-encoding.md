### F2: GenusData.β : M →+ M (PropN.lean L105-106) and its justification (pn-design.md §2 rows M and β, L103 and L111; §5 risk 1, L187-194; Summary item 5, L44-47)

**Finding.** F5 (ℍ'_n/ℚ is Galois, not printed by TYZ) is not needed for β : M →+ M, because M is existentially quantified. Take M := the ℤ[i]-submodule of A(ℍ'_n) generated by A[(1+i)³] and the Z(e) for e ∣ n, e ≡ 5, 7. Lemma 3.21(1),(4) (L2504-2516) gives Z(e)^{β'} = −Z(e) + s_e with s_e ∈ A[(1+i)³]. β'([u]x) = [u]β'(x) for u ∈ ℤ[i], since [i] is defined over ℚ(i), which β' fixes (F4; nt-tyz §2.2). So β'(Σ u_e Z(e) + t) = −Σ u_e Z(e) + Σ u_e s_e + t ∈ M. M ⊂ A(ℍ'_n): for e ≡ 5, H'_e is a factor of ℍ'_n (L1321); for e ≡ 7, Z(e) is defined over L_e (L2558). So tors_mem_range follows from Lemma 3.18 (L2230-2232), and torsion_of_zero is a property of the element P(n) itself. TYZ need F5 at L2593-2595 only because their X is an arbitrary element of A(ℍ'_n); finalStep's X is any element of the chosen M.
