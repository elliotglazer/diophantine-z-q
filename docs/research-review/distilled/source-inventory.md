# Source-application inventory for "Z is Diophantine in Q", Version 17

Consolidated from the repository's machine inventories (see section 4 for files and limits). This is a reading aid. It records what the inventories say was read and used; it is not a verification of any application.

**Column key**

- **Version read**: what the newest inventory covering the use records as the inspected text.
- **Uses**: `D` = number of `published_external` supplier records for this work among the 1,190 reconciled uses in the Version 16 dossier (title variants merged by hand). `C` = number of `\cite` occurrences of the work's bibliography key(s) in `manuscripts/quartic_v17.tex`. `A0xx` = application family in the 5 November audit, given only where the dossier has no external record.
- **Where**: labels of the manuscript sections whose dossier coverage unit contains the use. The dossier's units are line ranges of `quartic_v16.tex`; every unit's opening section title also occurs in `quartic_v17.tex`. Two units are long and span several sections: "deg8 unit" starts at `sec:degree8-original` (it contains the five-contact height argument) and "quartics unit" starts at `sec:quartics`.
- "not recorded" means the field is absent from the inventories I read.

## 4. Method and limits

**Files used.** The bibliography of `manuscripts/quartic_v17.tex` (154 `\bibitem` entries, parsed by script; `\cite` counts are occurrences per key, summed over duplicate keys for one work). `research/version16/protocol/DOSSIER.json` (1,190 reconciled uses: 404 `published_external`, 402 internal, 362 inline, 2 computation, 20 without supplier); its `publication` fields give authors, title, venue, version, locator and corrections, and its `coverage_index` gives line ranges that I mapped to section labels in `quartic_v16.tex`. `research/quartic214/PRIMARY_SOURCES.json` for the 214 family's height, bridge and modularity sources and access findings. `docs/theorem-application-audit/` (`sources.json`, `applications.json`, `coverage.json`, `FINDINGS.md`, `DEPENDENCIES.md`, `OPEN_ITEMS.md`) for the 5 November baseline. `research/version16/FINDINGS.json` and `protocol/CURRENT-CORRECTIONS.md`. `research/version14/proofs/direct-p5-modularity-v1/SOURCE-STATUS.md`, `cj-conductor-one-v1/CURRENT-SOURCE-STATUS-005.md`, `a001-moduli-current-v1/ADVERSE-CASE.md`, and `docs/research-review/historical-ceggps-status.md`. `research/version17/README.md`, `ARTIFACT-INDEX.md` and `CURRENT_VERSION17.md` for scope.

**How counts were derived.** `D` counts are dossier supplier records grouped by publication title; I merged title variants by hand (the 404 records carry 129 distinct title strings, which I reduced to 88 works). One record names two works jointly and is counted for both. The remaining 16 rows are works cited in the V17 bibliography, or listed in the 214 primary-source file, that have no external supplier record in the dossier. The Stacks Project is one row although it stands for about 40 separately pinned tags.

**What I could not reconcile or did not check.**

- The Version 16 dossier is the newest full source-use inventory, but it is bound to `quartic_v16.tex`. The 214-family native review dossiers (39 and 40 uses) contain no `published_external` suppliers at all: they point to internal suppliers, so the 214 family's published inputs come only from `PRIMARY_SOURCES.json`, which is an author record awaiting independent review by its own statement.
- Nine works with dossier use records are absent from the V17 bibliography (rows 42, 47, 70, 78, 81, 87, 89, 92, 93); the 2014 erratum in row 59 and the U^3 paper in row 61 are present but uncited. Conversely, rows 2, 3, 27, 45, 53, 54, 90 and 96–100 are cited in V17 but have no external supplier record, so their "statements used" come from the bibliography text or the 5 November audit.
- The bibliography key `gz` is Gross–Zagier 1986, while the dossier's only Gross–Zagier record is the 1985 "On singular moduli". I could not tell whether the 1986 use is covered elsewhere.
- Counts differ by inventory and are not comparable: the 5 November audit has 72 source records, 62 cited works and 82 application families; the Version 14 "136" figure counts selected proof-source files, not publications; I did not open `docs/manuscript-revisions/version14/final-coverage/CONNECTIONS.json` and did not verify the "1,042 uses" figure. I also did not open `INVENTORY.md`, `SOURCES.md`, the 9.5 MB `CONNECTIONS.json`, or any file named `PRIMARY_SOURCE_BRIDGES.md` or `FULL_COVER_SOURCE_PROFILES.json` (neither is at the top level of `research/quartic214/`).
- "Version read" changed between inventories for at least Kolyvagin, Poonen–Stoll, Baba–Granath, Buzzard, Voight, Pazuki and GTZ. I report the newest record and note the older one.
- Section labels are the first label in each dossier coverage unit. Two units are long, so "deg8 unit" and "quartics unit" are coarse. I did not re-locate uses in V17 line numbers.
- Many version fields are only a file hash. Where the inventory gives a hash and nothing about edition, I wrote "pinned PDF (hash only)".
- The inventories themselves state that source pinning and scoped reviews do not certify the applications, semantic exhaustiveness, or the main theorem. Nothing here upgrades that.
