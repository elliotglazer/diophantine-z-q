# Prose and unnamed input reconciliation

These entries are part of the direct-use supplement. They were identified while reading the proof, before the historical inventory was consulted. Naming a supplier below does not mean that every unnamed standard fact has been re-proved as a separate published theorem.

1. **Thin archimedean box, 16687–16695, GAU-004.** The required density is O(tau), while derivatives of the majorant may depend on tau. The source integrates in radial norm variables, whose difference has derivative bounded away from zero on the fixed positive norm range. This is an inline coarea/change-of-variables estimate. It does not assert uniform tau-dependent error constants.

2. **One-position medium sieve, 16763–16773, GAU-005.** This is a direct three-dimensional box-count input, not BL's five-dimensional theorem. For good ell and w nonzero, the k norm equations have disjoint zero sets. The distinguished norm-zero fibre has ell+(ell-1)chi elements, and the other fibres ell-chi. With the w=0 fibre included, the density is `(k+chi)/ell+O_k(ell^-2)`. The obstruction has a=0 and b²=-1, hence at most 2ell triples and density O(ell^-2). For any union of residues modulo squarefree m in a fixed smooth B-box, elementary lattice counting gives error bounded by `O(B²m+Bm²+m³)` after summing at most m³ classes, because each class contributes volume/m³ plus `O((B/m)²+B/m+1)`. In the actual range `m=ell[d,e] <= B^.03`, the Selberg double sum and ell sum remain much smaller than B^2.7. The same prime-harmonic and weighted-sieve calculation gives dimension k. Thus the short prose has a concrete supplier; treating it as an automatic application of BL would be wrong.

3. **Large-prime lattice basis, 16880–16913, GAU-007.** The basis comparable to Euclidean minima and the minima-product/determinant comparison are substantive geometric inputs. They are used to enclose the original B-box by coordinate sides O(B/lambda_i), volume O(B³/q²), and minimum side at least a constant B/T. They are also present in the BHB accepted proof, pp.16–18, extraction 692–832, examined by the prior tail review. The new graph retains them explicitly under BHB's proof method; the BHB lemma labels alone do not spell out this transition.

4. **Prime tail and divisor estimates, GAU-005/007/008.** Chebyshev's binomial argument is written in the medium proof and supplies the later sum over primes ell>L of ell^-2. The ordinary divisor bound and Rankin product bound supply the fixed-coordinate count. The divisor estimate follows by separating finitely many small primes in the prime factorization; `(e+1) <= C_epsilon p^(epsilon e)` gives tau(n) bounded by C_epsilon n^epsilon. The Rankin computation is displayed with its fixed exceptional prime set. These are retained as direct inputs; the sieve inventory's main named theorem does not replace them.

5. **Nilsequence geometry, GAU-015/022/026/030.** Nonzero horizontal characters push Haar measure to circle Haar measure; this and the rational-basis metric comparison give the short-arc obstruction. The rational conjugation step requires the lattice-index and metric estimates of GT Orbits Appendix A and the quantitative claim on p.547 of the published Mobius paper. The actual quotient is gamma_j^-1 G' gamma_j divided by its intersection with Gamma. These are not isometry claims about the old quotient.

6. **Ordinary majorant interval mean, GAU-037.** The constant-shift factor is either one or a single ordinary majorant. The source uses its mean on an interval of fixed relative length. The GT divisor-sum/linear-forms estimate on that interval supplies this input. The polynomial second moment concerns the remaining shifts with independent m,m', and weighted Cauchy–Schwarz supplies localization. This is not a pointwise logarithmic bound on the shrinking strip.

7. **Finite private-coordinate consumers, GAU-057/061/065/066/067/070.** Statements that eliminated inverses, powers or norm coordinates have no other consumers are computational assertions about the pinned straight-line program. They are not consequences of the displayed local algebra alone. The existing coefficient/elimination certificates and their independent review are the proposed suppliers. This pass records the dependence and preserves the old evidence scope; it did not replay those computations.

These refinements add explicit source-use granularity. No newly required arithmetic theorem or failed inference follows merely because an older graph stored them under a broader node.
