## Interfaces checked in the actual proof

The Gaussian ideal-to-prime conversion keeps two different factors distinct: four generators per Gaussian ideal in the norm-transfer sum, and two conjugate norm-p ideals per eligible rational prime. The centered weight uses the same conditional density as the final singular series.

The norm-transfer input is over arbitrary intervals and congruences. Type II requires distribution of the entire product orbit; mean-zero factors alone do not imply it. Integer dilation recombination handles large shifts by integer denominator clearing and handles small dilation ranges separately. Affine interpolation constructs the sequence on the full integer domain before applying a theorem on `[WN+b]`. The factorization proof subtracts the Haar mean on the conjugated quotient, accounts for its commensurable lattice, and sums the full-N error over every cell. Its choice of B precedes the upper polylogarithmic exponent.

The auxiliary linear-replacement cutoff and the later polynomial cutoff are distinct. The inverse theorem uses the full cyclic majorant norm `U^(2s)` for target `U^s`; the function on the interval is scaled by a fixed constant. The final bounded model is divided by a fixed bound before the theorem that assumes absolute value at most one. Boundary localization uses two independent polynomial variables for the second moment. The final field is fixed before any asymptotic limit. The obstruction estimate is compared with the ray-density lower bound before choosing the final sequence of size thresholds.
